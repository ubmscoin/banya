ubms ai 1: The Banya Number - A Two-Dimensional Number System and LLM Computation without a GPU

Author: Hyukjin Han - Independent Researcher, Hwaseong, Gyeonggi-do, Republic of Korea; bokkamsun@gmail.com

Abstract

Every nonzero number can be written in the form e^(ln x). Starting from this, the present paper builds an integer-only arithmetic system that runs a large language model (LLM) without multiplication and without a GPU. The paper proposes three things. First, the banya number. A number is two-dimensional by nature. A number that holds magnitude and angle as a single unit is called a banya number. Weights and activations are all banya numbers. Every operation is a unitary rotation, so there is neither conversion nor loss. Second, an expression itself is held as a value. The sum of banya numbers is not collapsed into a real number but preserved as an integer coefficient array over exponent terms of e. Because e is transcendental, equality comparison is exact on the coefficient array alone. Third, differentiation is rotation. The exponential form of e keeps its form under differentiation, and one differentiation is a 90-degree rotation. A fractional derivative is a rotation by an arbitrary angle, the same structure as the fractional-derivative rotation of the Banya Framework. As a result, the entire LLM pipeline runs with additions, shifts, and comparisons only, with no multiplier and no floating-point unit.
