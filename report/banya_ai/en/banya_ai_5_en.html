Cache-Dictionary Tokenization and Dynamic Embedding: Near-Free Vocabulary Expansion via a Low-Rank Head

Author: Hyukjin Han · ORCID 0009-0007-4132-1707 · Banya Research Series, Part 5 · bokkamsun@gmail.com

Abstract

Tokenization—splitting text into the word units a model handles—is arranged in two layers. The lower layer consists of syllable atoms (Korean phonetic units), and the upper layer is a bundle dictionary that holds frequent words as whole tokens. The vocabulary of the measured model stacks 8000 bundles on top of 2724 syllable atoms, for a total of 10724. Ordinarily, increasing the vocabulary fourfold makes the output head proportionally more expensive in parameters, but this paper makes vocabulary expansion nearly free by giving the output head a low-rank form. At vocabulary 10724, channel width 1024, and rank 128, the low-rank head has about 1.5 million parameters, about 7.3 times fewer than the dense head's about 10.98 million.
