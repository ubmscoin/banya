World-First Developmental Curriculum Learning and the Emergence of Axis Structure

Author: Hyukjin Han · ORCID 0009-0007-4132-1707 · Banya Research Series, Part 4 · bokkamsun@gmail.com

Abstract

Humans develop a sense of perception, space, and logic before they learn language. This paper trains a language model with a developmental curriculum, a training order that proceeds from easy stages to hard ones, imitating that sequence. Before language, the model first forms sensory axes such as size, distance, and temperature (an axis is a scale that measures one property), and language is then laid on top. The central question is whether, without hand-coding an ontology (a structural map in which concepts occupy their own places), the training order alone leads the filters and embeddings to form an ontological axis structure by themselves.
