Abstract

Four axioms (orthogonality, atomicity, minimal cost, causality preservation) and one proposition define a discrete computational system. Because no free parameters enter the deduction, fitting is excluded; because the axioms close the search space, numerological tuning is blocked. The present paper is the third in the Axiomatic Model series and proceeds on the same axiom set as Paper I and Paper II. The complete axiomatic system comprises 15 axioms; the present paper additionally uses its number registry (complete descriptive degrees of freedom), its recovery rule, and its juim-recording rule. The paper treats three structural constants of black hole thermodynamics. First, the coefficient 4 of the Bekenstein-Hawking entropy S = A/(4 l_P^2) is the structural-bracket domain count 4, while the 4 of the quasinormal-mode (QNM) asymptotic damping spacing M dw_I = 1/4 is the cost-bracket ball value 4 (= 1 time + 3 space) - the two 4s are different quantities registered in different brackets, coinciding numerically only in three spatial dimensions (Theorem 1). A discrimination criterion follows: the ball value varies as 1+d with spatial dimension d, whereas the entropy 4 is dimension-independent, so higher-dimensional holography separates the two readings. As auxiliary results we organize, taking the results of Paper II as input, the Barbero-Immirzi gamma = sqrt(3) ln3/8 (a definite deviation of 0.136% from the transcendental-equation root of the literature), the LQG area gap, and the QNM real part, and we show that the well-known mismatch between the LQG area quantum and Hod's area quantum reduces, after cancellation of ln3, to the single geometric factor 3pi/8. The derivation yields 4 unique predictions with explicit falsification conditions (Table 6).
