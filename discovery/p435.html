$$\nu=\frac{p}{q},\ q\leq 7=\text{CAS 완전자유도},\ \nu\in\left\{\tfrac{1}{3},\tfrac{2}{5},\tfrac{3}{7},\ldots\right\}\ (q\ \text{홀수}\leq 7)$$
