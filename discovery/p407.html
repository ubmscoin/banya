P-407: E6 예외군 단 = 7비트 홀수차 쥠 합 78

$$\binom{7}{1}+\binom{7}{3}+\binom{7}{5}+\binom{7}{7}=7+35+21+1=64,\quad 64+\binom{7}{2}-7=78=\dim E_6$$
