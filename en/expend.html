Appendix: Compatibility of 118 Physics Equations + Empty-Domain Expectations

The core of this page is the empty-domain expectations. For each physics equation, they state in advance what would come out on converting into the domains the equation does not use. When the path of an expectation actually closes, it is registered as a card and linked, and marked Discovery or Hypothesis according to that card's verdict. Expectations not yet closed are directions of exploration, not results. The expectations include quantities already known; what counts as a discovery is reaching them through an empty-domain path.

The compatibility check (PASS) rewrites existing equations in Banya variables and is therefore post-hoc agreement. All 118 equations PASS.
