Core Finding

H-367: M_W/M_Z = √(23/30) = 0.87560 (CAS access path ratio)

$$M_W/M_Z=\sqrt{23/30}=0.87560$$

(1) The W-boson to Z-boson mass ratio is M_W/M_Z = √(23/30) = 0.87560. This is derived from the ratio of CAS access paths.

(2) Banya formula: M_W/M_Z = √(23/30) = 0.87560. W has only crossing access (30 - 7 = 23), Z has full access (30).

(3) By Axiom 1 (4 axes), N = 30 is the total number of access paths. 7 = C(7,1) = single-DOF access. The W boson can only have cross-domain access (30 - 7 = 23), while the Z boson has full access (30).

(4) Structural consequence: juim (mass) is proportional to the cumulative write count (Axiom 6). Fewer access paths mean fewer cumulative writes and smaller juim. The reason M_W < M_Z is that W has 7 fewer access paths than Z.

(5) Numerical values: √(23/30) = √0.7667 = 0.87560. Experimental value M_W/M_Z = 80.379/91.188 = 0.88148. Error ≈ 0.7%.

(6) Consistency: cos θ_W = M_W/M_Z ≈ 0.8815 (experiment). 0.87560 is the tree-level approximation, and including one-loop corrections it can approach the experimental value.

(7) Physical correspondence: the Weinberg angle sin²θ_W = 1 − (M_W/M_Z)² = 1 − 23/30 = 7/30 = 0.2333. The precise value of sin²θ_W is derived separately in D-02.

(8) In the conventional Standard Model, M_W/M_Z = cos θ_W is the result of electroweak symmetry breaking. In the Banya Framework it is derived from geometric constraints on CAS access paths.

5-Step Recursive Substitution: Deriving M_W/M_Z from the CAS Access-Path Ratio

Step 1. Banya Formula

$$\delta^2 = (\text{time} + \text{space})^2 + (\text{observer} + \text{superposition})^2$$

The juim of the W and Z bosons is proportional to the cumulative write count (Axiom 6). Writes happen over the access paths opened by the 4 domain axes (Axiom 1), so the mass ratio of the two bosons is read as the ratio of the access paths each can write over.

Step 2. Norm Substitution

The norm being substituted is the access-path ratio norm $23/30$. The total access paths are $N=30$ and the single-DOF accesses are $C(7,1)=7$; Z writes over all 30 paths while W writes only over the 23 cross accesses left after removing the 7 single-DOF ones — hence this norm. Since juim enters on a square-root scale of cumulative writes, the mass ratio is $\sqrt{23/30}$.

$M_W/M_Z=\cos\theta_W$
$M_W/M_Z=\sqrt{23/30}$
