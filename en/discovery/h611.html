It is chosen because the non-commuting terms $H_k$ cannot be processed together in one cycle, so the CAS divides them into sequential Compares; the residual that arises is the Trotter error. Why this norm: the Trotter error $O(t^2/n)$ itself originates from the CAS self-reference emulation cost, so only a norm that carries that very cost structure maps onto it without slack.
