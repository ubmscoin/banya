5-Step Recursive Substitution: Deriving the Stark Second-Order Shift $-\tfrac{1}{2}\alpha_p E^2$ from the Domain-Bit Shift Cost

Step 1. Banya Formula

$$\delta^2 = (\text{time} + \text{space})^2 + (\text{observer} + \text{superposition})^2$$

The Stark shift is the asymmetric shift cost that an external electric field adds to the domain bits (bit 0-3). Since the time axis holds the electric component (Axiom 1) and its change rides on the domain bits (Axiom 5), the asymmetric shift is read as a $\delta^2$ cost increment of the Banya formula.

Step 2. Norm Substitution

(a) The norm being substituted is the domain-bit norm — symmetric to the Zeeman shift riding on the lock bits, the Stark shift rides on the domain bits (bit 0-3). (b) This norm is chosen because in non-degenerate states the Compare branch does not close at first order, so the field adds cost only at second order (Axiom 7: first order if degenerate, second order if non-degenerate). The energy shift is therefore substituted by the product of the polarizability $\alpha_p$ and the squared field strength.

$\Delta E^{(2)} = -\dfrac{1}{2}\displaystyle\sum_{k\neq n}\dfrac{|\langle k| eEz |n\rangle|^2}{E_k - E_n}$
$\Delta E_{\text{Stark}} = -\dfrac{1}{2}\,\alpha_p\,E_{\text{ext}}^2$
$\Delta E_{\text{Stark}}$: Stark shift | $\alpha_p$: polarizability = domain-bit response cost | $E_{\text{ext}}$: external field strength | 2nd order: non-degenerate branch (Axiom 7)

Step 3. Constant Substitution

$E_{ext}$ = external electric field strength
$\alpha_{p}$ = polarizability (domain-bit response)
W(n=2) = first-order Stark (degenerate), dE $\sim$ e a E

Step 4. Domain Transformation

(a) The transformation is into the domain-bit shift domain (electric-field response). (b) What it reveals is whether the energy shift of a non-degenerate level is linear in the squared field strength. The hydrogen $n=2$ degenerate level splits at first order $\Delta E \propto eaE$, the non-degenerate level at second order $\propto E^2$.

$\Delta E_{\text{Stark}} = -\dfrac{1}{2}\,\alpha_p\,E_{\text{ext}}^2 \quad(\text{non-degenerate})$
$\Delta E_{\text{Stark}} \propto e\,a\,E_{\text{ext}} \quad(n=2\ \text{degenerate})$
