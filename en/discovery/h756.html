5-Step Recursive Substitution: Deriving the Ergodic Condition from the δ-Polling Traversal Cost

Step 1. Banya Formula

$$\delta^2 = (\text{time} + \text{space})^2 + (\text{observer} + \text{superposition})^2$$

Ergodicity is the cost question of whether, as δ (Axiom 15: the global flag on the left of the equals sign) polls every tick and traverses the FSM states (Axiom 14: the R·C·S·IDLE cycle) along the time axis, its accumulated cost (Axiom 4) is distributed evenly over the whole state space.

Step 2. Norm Substitution

The norm being substituted is the time-average norm of δ polling. The ergodic condition is "the long-time average of one trajectory = the ensemble average over state space," and if δ revisits every finite FSM state without omission (Axiom 14 finiteness) these two averages coincide — that is why this norm is chosen. The time average of an observable $f$ is therefore substituted by the cumulative sum over the δ-polling trajectory.

$\bar{f}_{\text{time}} = \lim_{T\to\infty}\dfrac{1}{T}\int_0^T f(x_t)\,dt$
$\bar{f}_{\delta} = \lim_{N\to\infty}\dfrac{1}{N}\sum_{n=1}^{N} f(s_n)$
$\bar{f}_{\text{time}}$: time average | $\bar{f}_{\delta}$: δ-polling $N$-tick cumulative average | $s_n$: FSM state at tick $n$ (Axiom 14) | $N$: number of polling ticks
