H-06: Derivation of the exponent 57

Origin of the exponent 57 in the D-15 cosmological-constant formula Λ·l_p² = α⁵⁷. 57 = C(7,2) + C(7,3) + C(7,0) = 21 + 35 + 1. It is the sum of specific-grade components of the 7-dimensional exterior algebra.

In δ² = (t+s)² + (o+sp)², combining the 4 domains (t, s, o, sp) with the 3 CAS stages (R, C, S) gives total DOF = 7 (Axiom 9). In the exterior algebra Λ*(R⁷) over these 7 DOF, the grade-2 component C(7,2) = 21, the grade-3 component C(7,3) = 35, and the grade-0 component C(7,0) = 1 sum to 57.

Axiom 9 (complete description DOF = 4 domains + 3 CAS internal = 7) ==> Axiom 1 (4 domains of the Banya formula). 7 is the fundamental count of the framework. 57 = 21 + 35 + 1 is the unique combinatorial decomposition from 7.

The grade-2 component (21) = pairwise-interaction count among DOFs. The grade-3 component (35) = three-body-interaction count. The grade-0 (1) = existence of the system itself (δ). The cosmological constant is fixed by the total of these three structural contributions.

α⁵⁷ ≈ 10⁻¹²³. Compared with the Planck-unit cosmological constant value Λ·l_p² ≈ 10⁻¹²², a factor of ≈ 1.8 remains. This factor is resolved by H-16 as e^(21/35) = 1.822.

Λ·l_p² = α⁵⁷ × e^(21/35) matches the observed value within 0.09%. The 122-digit fine-tuning problem is solved at an error of 0.09%.

The cosmological constant problem (the 10¹²⁰ gap between QFT prediction and observation) is one of the largest unsolved problems in physics. In the Banya Framework, it is derived from the single structure α⁵⁷.

The string-theory landscape relies on the anthropic principle for selecting one vacuum out of 10⁵⁰⁰. The Banya Framework derives the exponent 57 from the 7-dimensional exterior algebra, so the anthropic principle is unnecessary.

If the decomposition of 57 can be other than 21+35+1, the uniqueness weakens. Settled if it is proven that no other decomposition exists, or if the 21/35 ratio is independently verified (H-16).

There is room to make the combinatorial derivation path of 57 more rigorous. A CAS-structural argument for why grades 2+3+0 are selected and grades 1 (7) and 4 (35) are excluded is needed.
