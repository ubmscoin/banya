In the Banya formula $\delta^2=(\text{time}+\text{space})^2+(\text{observer}+\text{superposition})^2$, the DOF of quantum error correction arise from the 7-bit full-description DOF (Axiom 9, P-66).

The fault-tolerant QEC threshold is the limit below which the logical error rate can be reduced arbitrarily if the physical error rate is lower.

This prediction holds that the theoretical ceiling of this threshold is $1/C(7,3)=1/35\approx2.86\%$.

35 = $C(7,3)$ is the number of ways to choose three slots from 7 bits (the same integer as the baryon ceiling P-58), arising from the CAS 3-step combinatorial structure.

So the threshold is the reciprocal of the CAS 3-step combination count, and QEC succeeds if the physical error rate is below $1/35$ ==> threshold ceiling $1/35$.
