Core Finding

H-966: Sequence-Targeted Cut (CRISPR·Restriction Enzyme) = Compare-true-Gated Irreversible Swap

$$\text{sequence match} = \text{Compare-true} \;\to\; \text{cut (Swap, irreversible)},\qquad \text{no match} = \text{Compare-false} \;\to\; \text{hold (dissociate)}$$

Structural correspondence revealed by domain transformation — sequence complementary match=Compare-true licenses an irreversible double-strand cut (Swap/collapse); no match=Compare-false→hold/dissociate (no commit). Axiom 7 conditional branch + Axiom 2 irreversibility. Quantitative unfinished → hypothesis.

Match-gated irreversible write (Axiom 7). A CRISPR-Cas9 gRNA base-pairs its ~20-nt spacer to a PAM-adjacent complementary target and cleaves both strands only on a correct match. Restriction enzymes likewise bind a specific (often palindromic) site, then cut — recognition is separate from and gates the cut. This is Axiom 7 exactly: match (Compare-true) ⟹ irreversible cut (Swap = collapse), mismatch (Compare-false) ⟹ dissociate with no commit (hold). The cut's irreversibility is Axiom 2.

Why it passed (not a duplicate). Recognition alone would duplicate the carded "complementary pairing = Compare." But what this card catches is the match-gated irreversible write (Axiom 7 conditional collapse), which is not yet carded. The irreversibility of the cut is a cleaner Swap than reversible recognition-gated signaling, so it does not reduce to generic enzyme specificity. No number-matching, no evolutionary-contingency dependence, no undischarged quantitative claim.

Re-substitution purpose: Re-substitution purpose: complements H-964 (tRNA Axiom 7 branch); a biology case of the Axiom 7 conditional collapse.

Why the Hypothesis Halted: Held as hypothesis (Class III). Remaining tasks: (1) quantify the Compare threshold, (2) map off-target = Compare-false leakage.
