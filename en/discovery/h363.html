Core Finding

H-363: Nibble entropy sum = 4 ln2 + 3 ln2 = 7 ln2

$$S_{\text{total}}=S_{\text{domain}}+S_{\text{operator}}=4\ln 2+3\ln 2=7\ln 2$$

(1) The total entropy of the two nibbles is 7 ln 2, the sum of the domain nibble's 4 ln 2 and the CAS nibble's 3 ln 2. Because the two nibbles are orthogonal (Axiom 1), the entropies are additive.

(2) Banya formula: S_total = S_domain + S_operator = 4 ln 2 + 3 ln 2 = 7 ln 2. This is the sum of the maximum entropy of the 4-bit domain and the maximum entropy of the 3-bit CAS.

(3) By Axiom 1 (4-axis orthogonality), the domain nibble and the CAS nibble are independent. By Axiom 9 (Pascal's triangle), the maximum entropy of the effective DOF 7 is 7 ln 2.

(4) Structural consequence: the domain 4-bit has 2⁴ = 16 states; the CAS 3-bit (counting only valid states) has 4 states (000, 001, 011, 111). However, the entropy calculation uses the maximum entropy 3 ln 2 of the 3-bit (the entire space, including invalid states).

(5) Numerical values: 7 ln 2 = 7 × 0.6931 = 4.852 nats = 7 bits. This is the maximum information per system tick.

(6) Consistency: in the Bekenstein-Hawking entropy S = A/(4l_P²), a black hole's information storage density is bits per area. 7 bits/tick is the maximum information density of a single entity.

(7) Physical correspondence: thermodynamic entropy S = k_B × 7 ln 2 per entity. This is the maximum entropy of a single entity (e.g., proton). A black hole is the entropy sum of multiple entities.

(8) Including δ (bit 7) gives 8 bits, but since δ is fixed at 1, the entropy contribution of δ is 0. Therefore the total entropy = 7 ln 2 + 0 = 7 ln 2.

(9) Verification: measure the thermodynamic entropy of a single proton and confirm that it approaches 7 ln 2 × k_B. Also verify that the 7-bit information limit is consistent with the holographic principle.

(10) Remaining tasks: the entropy contribution of invalid CAS states (010, 100, 101, 110), the additivity rule for the entropy of multi-entity systems, and the quantitative connection between 7 ln 2 and black-hole entropy are pending.
