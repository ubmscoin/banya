P-640. The Riemann hypothesis holds: every nontrivial zero has $\mathrm{Re}\,\rho = 1/2$

Key finding

P-640: under the orthogonal = independent proposition every oscillatory term of the explicit formula must share the orthogonal-norm amplitude $x^{1/2}$ -- there are no zeros off the critical line

$$\zeta(\rho) = 0,\; 0 \lt \mathrm{Re}\,\rho \lt 1 \;\Longrightarrow\; \mathrm{Re}\,\rho = \tfrac12$$

Prediction (pending) -- shared with the majority view in mathematics, so discriminating power is low. The first trillions of zeros are numerically confirmed on the critical line

H-996 read the critical exponent 1/2 as the norm exponent of $x$ independent orthogonal units (Axiom 1 proposition orthogonal = independent; Axiom 2 proposition norm $\sqrt N$). If even one zero $\rho = \beta + i\gamma$ with $\beta \gt 1/2$ lay off the line, the amplitude $x^\beta$ of its term $x^\rho$ would outgrow the norm exponent. That would mean the fluctuation of the integer staircase exceeds the norm scale of independent orthogonal units, incompatible with the reading of H-996. The framework therefore predicts that the Riemann hypothesis holds.

Re-substitution use: falsification anchor for the Riemann bundle H-995, H-996, H-997. If an off-line zero is found, the whole bundle is re-examined.

Awaiting verification: this card is a prediction on the truth of the Riemann hypothesis and is pending. The chain of support is Axiom 1, Axiom 2, Axiom 3; it is not a proof. Discarded on numerical confirmation of an off-line zero.
