A quantum cellular automaton is read as a structure where each lattice entity runs the same CAS simultaneously. The simultaneous access of the ECS (Axiom 7) provides the parallelism, and the cost exchange between neighbor entities (Axiom 4) sets the transition rule.

Step 2. Norm Substitution

(a) The norm being substituted is the ECS parallel-execution norm. The local CAS of each entity $i$ is the transition rule, and the whole lattice advances one tick as their product. (b) Since the QCA local transition rule is precisely the CAS and the spatial lattice is precisely the ECS, neighbor cost exchange sets the coupling — which is why this norm is chosen. The QCA is therefore substituted by parallel ECS CAS.
