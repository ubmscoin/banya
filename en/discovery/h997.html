H-997. Zero-counting constant $7/8 = 1 - (1/4)(1/2)$: Stirling phase decomposition

Key finding

H-997: the constant 7/8 of the Riemann-von Mangoldt formula is the pole share 1 minus the share $1/8$ of the Stirling phase $-\pi/8$, and $1/8$ splits into the product of reciprocals of the listed numbers 4 and 2

$$N(T) = \frac{T}{2\pi}\log\frac{T}{2\pi} - \frac{T}{2\pi} + \frac78 + S(T) + O(T^{-1}), \qquad \frac78 = 1 + \frac{1}{\pi}\Big(\tfrac14 - \tfrac12\Big)\frac{\pi}{2} = 1 - \frac14\cdot\frac12$$

Hypothesis -- the decomposition is mathematically exact but its Banya reading is not unique. The reading 7/8 = CAS 7 / d-ring 8 uses 8, a number dropped from the list, and is quarantined at class III or below

The zero count is $N(T) = \theta(T)/\pi + 1 + S(T)$, and the constant term $-\pi/8$ comes from the Stirling expansion of the Riemann-Siegel theta $\theta(T) = \arg\Gamma(\tfrac14 + \tfrac{iT}{2}) - \tfrac{T}{2}\log\pi$. Its origin is the imaginary part $(\tfrac14 - \tfrac12)\arg z$ of the Stirling form $(z - \tfrac12)\log z$ at $z = \tfrac14 + \tfrac{iT}{2}$, with $\arg z \to \pi/2$ as $T\to\infty$. Hence $-\pi/8 = (\tfrac14-\tfrac12)\cdot\tfrac{\pi}{2}$; dividing by $\pi$ gives $-1/8$, and adding the pole share $+1$ gives $7/8$. Here $\tfrac14$ is the critical line $\mathrm{Re}\,s = \tfrac12$ folded in half by $\Gamma(s/2)$.

Two candidate Banya readings, side by side. (a) $1/8 = (1/4)(1/2)$: 4 and 2 are listed numbers (Axiom 9), and $\pi/2$ equals the arc of the partial crossing that the Axiom 2 proposition (geometric range of the CAS forward path) calls "incomplete, crossing only 1 of the 3 boundaries". (b) $7/8$ = CAS internal degrees of freedom 7 / d-ring 8 bits: 7 is listed, but 8 is decomposed as $2^3$ and dropped by the filter of the Axiom 2 proposition (CAS data types). Neither reading is forced by the combination grammar (shift inside a bracket, + across brackets; Axiom 9 proposition).

Re-substitution use: together with the domain-time frequency density $\tfrac{1}{2\pi}\log\tfrac{T}{2\pi}$ of H-995, material for a full Banya reading of the zero-counting formula. Collects a case of the partial crossing $\pi/2$ appearing in an actual quantity.

Why it stops at Hypothesis: the mathematical decomposition is exact, but assigning 4, 2 and $\pi/2$ to axiomatic places is a retrospective identification and not unique. Reading (b) uses the dropped number 8 and carries a high numerology risk. Verdict given before multiple-comparison correction.

Step 1. Banya equation

The Banya equation is a topology, not a metric (Axiom 1 proposition), and in its unit-circle form one + crossing is a $\pi$ rotation. This card reads the constant term of the zero count as a quotient by the phase unit $\pi$.

Step 2. Norm substitution

$N(T) = \theta(T)/\pi + 1 + S(T)$, $\quad\theta(T) = \tfrac{T}{2}\log\tfrac{T}{2\pi} - \tfrac{T}{2} - \tfrac{\pi}{8} + O(T^{-1})$
$-\tfrac{\pi}{8} = \big(\tfrac14 - \tfrac12\big)\cdot\tfrac{\pi}{2} \;\Rightarrow\; \tfrac{1}{\pi}\big(-\tfrac{\pi}{8}\big) + 1 = \tfrac78$
$\tfrac14$: the $s = \tfrac12$ point of $\Gamma(s/2)$ | $\tfrac12$: Stirling $(z - \tfrac12)$ | $\tfrac{\pi}{2}$: asymptotic $\arg z$, candidate partial-crossing arc (Axiom 2 proposition) | $1$: pole share

Step 3. Constant substitution

$N(100) = 29.0023$ (main term + 7/8), actual count 29
$N(1000) = 648.616$, actual 649 (difference is the fluctuation $S(T)$)

Step 4. Domain conversion

Convert the zero count to the cumulative number of domain-time frequencies (H-995). The main term $\tfrac{T}{2\pi}\log\tfrac{T}{2\pi}$ is the cumulative density up to frequency $T$, and the constant $7/8$ is the origin correction of that accumulation. This card reads the origin correction as the difference between the pole 1 and the partial-crossing share $1/8$.

Step 5. Finding

Verdict: Hypothesis. Falsification: none (decomposition of a mathematical identity).
