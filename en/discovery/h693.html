H-693: Total Internal Reflection = Critical Angle of Cost Propagation Speed Ratio

$$\theta_c = \arcsin\!\left(\frac{n_2}{n_1}\right) \;\leftrightarrow\; \text{CAS cost-propagation boundary reflection condition}$$

Grade: B

Total internal reflection is the phenomenon in which CAS cost propagation, when incident from a high-density medium onto a low-density medium above the critical angle, has its cost fully bounced back at the boundary. The evanescent wave = exponential decay of CAS cost beyond the boundary.

H-692 (Snell's law), Axiom 4 (cost boundary)

H-692 (refractive index ratio), Axiom 4 (cost propagation impossibility = cost reflection), Axiom 7 (critical angle determined by ECS boundary condition)

Critical angle $\theta_c$ = the CAS cost-propagation limit at which the refraction angle becomes $90°$. Evanescent field = penetration depth of CAS cost at the boundary surface $\delta \propto \lambda / \sqrt{\sin^2\theta - n_{21}^2}$.

Glass-air: $\theta_c \approx 41.8°$. Diamond-air: $\theta_c \approx 24.4°$.

Consistent with optical-fiber and prism experiments.

5-Step Recursive Substitution: Deriving the Critical Angle from the Boundary Limit of the Cost-Propagation-Speed Ratio

Step 1. Banya Formula

$$\delta^2 = (\text{time} + \text{space})^2 + (\text{observer} + \text{superposition})^2$$

Total internal reflection is the cost in which a CAS cost wave cannot cross from a dense medium into a rarer one and is wholly returned at the boundary. It is read through the structure in which the limiting angle, where cost propagation becomes impossible, forces reflection (Axiom 4).

Step 2. Norm Substitution

The norm being substituted is the refractive-index-ratio norm of H-692 closed at the $90^\circ$ refraction-angle limit. It is chosen because, under the ECS boundary condition (Axiom 7), once the refraction angle exceeds $90^\circ$ the cost can no longer cross the boundary and is wholly returned. The critical angle is therefore substituted by the arcsine of the refractive-index ratio.

$n_1\sin\theta_c = n_2\sin 90^\circ$
$\theta_c = \arcsin\!\left(\dfrac{n_2}{n_1}\right)$
$\theta_c$: critical angle | $n_1$: dense-medium index | $n_2$: rare-medium index | $90^\circ$: refraction-angle limit = cost-propagation boundary (Axiom 4)

Step 3. Constant Substitution

n_glass   = 1.50
n_diamond = 2.417
n_air     = 1.0003      589 nm, room temperature

Step 4. Domain Transformation

The transformation is into the geometric-optics boundary domain. What it reveals is the critical angle at which light begins to be wholly returned at the glass-air boundary. Substituting the refractive-index ratio into the arcsine yields that angle.

$\theta_c = \arcsin\!\left(\dfrac{1.0003}{1.50}\right) = 41.8^\circ$
$\theta_c^{\text{diamond}} = \arcsin\!\left(\dfrac{1.0003}{2.417}\right) = 24.4^\circ$
