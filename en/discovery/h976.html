Minisuperspace truncates the full superspace under homogeneity/isotropy to the two variables scale factor $a$ and homogeneous scalar field $\phi$. It is a practice physicists confess works without justification. Why does the answer not go badly wrong when 6 dimensions (the superspace 6, precisely D-158) are cut to 2.
