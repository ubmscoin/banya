H-457: Schrödinger's Cat = RLU instability of macroscopic superposition

$$|\text{cat}\rangle = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(|\text{alive}\rangle + |\text{dead}\rangle) \;\xrightarrow{\tau_D \sim 10^{-30}\text{s}}\; \rho_{\text{mixed}}$$

Grade: B

The Schrödinger's cat thought experiment problematizes superposition of macroscopic objects. Banya frame: a macroscopic object interacts with $N_{\text{env}} \sim 10^{23}$ environmental entities (Axiom 11), so the RLU ranking of the superposition index drops instantly and the cache evicts it (H-445). Decoherence time $\tau_D \sim \hbar/(k_B T N_{\text{env}}) \sim 10^{-30}$ s. The cat is "in principle capable of superposition, but in practice loses it instantly".

Axiom 13 (superposition = ECS index), Axiom 11 (multiple projection ==> environment coupling)

Axiom 13 (ECS index superposition) ==> Axiom 11 ($N_{\text{env}}$ projection ==> RLU dispersion) ==> H-445 (RLU eviction = decoherence) ==> Axiom 7 (Compare ==> Swap determined)

The cat paradox does not exist. Superposition is not "impossible in principle" but "impossible to sustain". The ECS index of a macroscopic object has so many environmental projections that RLU eviction completes within 1 FSM tick. The micro-macro boundary is not sharp; it is a continuous transition where $\tau_D$ becomes shorter than observable time.

C₇₀ fullerene (Arndt 1999): $N \sim 70$, $\tau_D \sim 10^{-17}$ s ==> interference observable. Dust particle ($10^{18}$ atoms): $\tau_D \sim 10^{-31}$ s ==> interference impossible. Boundary: $N \sim 10^{6}$~$10^{9}$.

Consistent with fullerene and macromolecule interference experiments (Fein 2019, mass ~25,000 amu).
