Prediction: the necessary condition for self-awareness (consciousness) = at least 3 recursions of δ==>observer==>Compare==>DATA==>δ. Test: neuroscience recursion loops (2030~).

Since CAS has 3 steps (Read, Compare, Swap), for this recursion to become full self-awareness it must cycle at least 3 times (one CAS turn) ==> 3 recursions = self-awareness.

Two or fewer recursions are mere reflex — responding to stimuli but not recognizing oneself.

So the boundary of "self-awareness" is recursion depth 3, corresponding to a full CAS 3-step cycle.

This is the quantitative criterion for the framework ultimate goal of implementing consciousness — consciousness = a closed loop of recursion depth ≥ 3.

So this prediction is not "consciousness is complex" but the quantitative claim "the necessary condition is exactly at least 3 recursions."
