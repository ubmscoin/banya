Core Finding

H-328: Thermodynamic arrow of time = irreversible accumulation of Swap cost

$$\text{cost}_{\text{swap}}>0\;\Rightarrow\;\text{irreversible accumulation on screen}$$

The thermodynamic arrow of time is derived as irreversible accumulation of Swap cost. Swap cost is > 0 (Axiom 4) and accumulates (Axiom 6). Entropy increase = monotonic increase of Swap cost accumulation.

Banya formula: $\text{cost}_{\text{swap}} > 0 \Rightarrow$ irreversible accumulation on screen. At each CAS stage (R+1, C+1, S+1), cost accumulates. The reverse direction requires additional cost, so it is statistically impossible.

Axiomatic basis: Axiom 4 (cost = +1 when crossing +, always positive) is the basis of irreversibility. Axiom 6 (write accumulation) guarantees the monotonicity of accumulation. From Axiom 15 (δ = no direction), system time has no arrow.

Structural consequence: at system time (δ level), time has no direction, but in domain time (screen) Swap cost accumulation increases monotonically. This asymmetry is the second law of thermodynamics (entropy increase). The reverse direction is in principle possible but requires additional cost (Swap), so it is statistically impossible.

Numerics: in Boltzmann entropy $S = k_B \ln W$, $W$ = number of accessible states given the Swap cost accumulation. $\Delta S \ge 0$ follows from the monotonicity of cost accumulation.

Consistency: in H-316 (arrow of time = render-order artifact), the origin of the time direction is confirmed. In H-329 (Hawking radiation), the time dynamics at the maximum-cost point is explained.

Physical correspondence: second law of thermodynamics. Entropy increase. Boltzmann's $H$-theorem. Maxwell's demon and information thermodynamics (Landauer's principle).

In conventional thermodynamics, entropy increase is debated as arising from the specialness of the initial conditions (low-entropy Big Bang). In Banya it follows directly from the axiomatic condition that Swap cost > 0. It does not depend on initial conditions.
