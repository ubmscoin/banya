Step 2. Norm Substitution

(a) The norm being substituted is the RLU propagation-distance norm -- the strain norm whose amplitude is inversely proportional to the distance $r$. (b) The measured quantity is the length change read by the CAS Compare of the two arms' optical-path difference (Axiom 5), and that strain closes through the RLU distance $r$ (Axiom 6 RLU = propagation distance); hence the distance norm is chosen. The detected arm-length change is therefore substituted by strain $\times$ arm length. Why this norm: the Interferometer Arm-Length Change itself originates from the RLU Propagation Distance, so only a norm that carries that very cost structure maps onto it without slack. Why the substitution is valid: the standard physics form on the left and the Banya form on the right denote the same quantity, the latter merely rewriting it in CAS cost terms.

$h = \dfrac{\Delta L}{L} \propto \dfrac{1}{r}$
$\Delta L = h\,L$
$h$: strain (dimensionless) | $\Delta L$: arm-length change | $L$: interferometer arm length | $r$: RLU propagation distance (Axiom 6)
