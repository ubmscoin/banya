Electrical conduction is the process by which CAS cost (Axiom 4) is passed from one entity to the next. If the band gap (H-618) is 0, the cost flows without a gap, giving a conductor; if the gap is positive, the cost must cross the gap, giving an insulator. Conductor/insulator are the two gap-presence cases of the same FSM norm structure.

If the RLU thermal energy (Axiom 6) exceeds the gap, cost transfer opens; if smaller, it is blocked.
