Grade: B

The 3-series classification scheme of the Banya library. D-card (Discovery): a derivation confirmed by observation. H-card (Hypothesis): derived but awaiting independent confirmation. P-card (Prediction): a new prediction not yet observed. Cleanly partitions the scientific status of each card.

Status classification of the scientific method.

D-card: axiom ==> derivation ==> observational confirmation complete. Highest confidence. Examples: $\alpha$ derivation, quark mass ratios. H-card: axiom ==> derivation complete, awaiting observational confirmation or precision still insufficient. Example: physical interpretation of a specific ratio. P-card: axiom ==> new prediction. Verifiable by experiment. Examples: new particles, new effects.

Falsifiability: P-cards supply falsifiable predictions. D-cards: the verified foundation of the framework. H-cards: once mature, promoted/moved to D or P. Transitions across the 3 series: H==>D (observational confirmation), H==>P (new prediction derived).

D-cards: $\sim 150$. H-cards: $\sim 430+$. P-cards: $\sim 120$. Total: 696+.
