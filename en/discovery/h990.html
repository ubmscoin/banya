H-990: Signature ladder $(5,2)$-$(5,1)$-$(5,0)$ = two-stage elimination by observer self-exclusion and collapse

$(5,2)$ ==(observer excluded in principle)==> $(5,1)$ ==(superposition eliminated in execution = collapse)==> $(5,0)$

Hypothesis -- standing signature of the observable world = (5,1); settled record = (5,0) Euclidean. Candidate origin of Euclideanization (the Hartle-Hawking Euclidean segment, Wick rotation)

The backbone of the ladder is that the two reversible axes are eliminated in different ways. The observer is excluded in principle: what is observed is the output of the observer filter, and a filter cannot place itself on its own pass list (Axiom 10: the subject of access, never its object; Axiom 13: DATA access requires filter passage; Paper I Theorem 1 (B): reversible axes select but are not recorded as data). Superposition is eliminated in execution: when an observation actually executes (Compare true), collapse occurs and the superposition is recorded in DATA as a single settled state (Axiom 7).

Three rungs therefore stand: the whole that no one observes is $(5,2)$ (the extension that no observation captures in its entirety); the world an observer can see (excluding only itself) is $(5,1)$ (where the DeWitt metric resides, D-158); and the closed ledger in which observation has completed and even superposition is settled into record is $(5,0)$ with no negative axis, the Euclidean signature. The intuition that a recorded past is static is expressed as a signature, and a candidate seat appears for why the no-boundary proposal draws the beginning segment of the universe as a Euclidean 4-geometry (H-967) and why Wick rotation (the standard technique of computing in Euclidean signature by making the time axis imaginary) works: Euclideanization is read not as a computational trick but as a move to the signature of settled record.
