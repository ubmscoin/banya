H-962: DNA Molecular Structure = Banya-Framework Right-Hand-Side (FSM) Component Mapping

$$\text{Base 4} \leftrightarrow \text{Domain 4-axis},\quad \text{A}{=}00 \leftrightarrow \text{T}{=}11\ (\text{conjugate}),\quad \|\delta\cdot\bar\delta\| = \cos^2\theta+\sin^2\theta = 1$$

Structural correspondence revealed by domain transformation — 4 bases = 4-axis, complementary pairing = Compare, complementarity = bit-inversion = conjugate (original × conjugate norm closure), 2 strands = data + reference. Quantitative step (integers derived from axioms) unfinished → hypothesis.

This card maps DNA's molecular components onto the Banya-framework right-hand side (the Axiom 1–14 FSM machine). (1) The four bases (A·T·G·C) = the four domain axes (Axiom 1), and the {A-T}{G-C} grouping of 4 into 2 pairs has the same form as the two brackets. (2) Complementary pairing (bind if matched) = the matching judgment of CAS Compare (Axiom 2).

Why complementarity is "inversion": under the 2-bit encoding A=00, T=11, C=01, G=10, complementary bases are bitwise NOT of each other. This has the same form as the norm closure of the Axiom 1 proposition (unit-circle form), where the product of the original $\delta$ and its conjugate $\bar\delta$ closes the norm $\|\delta\cdot\bar\delta\|=\cos^2\theta+\sin^2\theta=1$ — the original strand and the complementary (conjugate) strand meet and close the information. Note: "complementarity = NOT" is the representation given our 2-bit encoding; the molecule itself binds by shape complementarity (chemistry).

2 strands = Compare's data + reference (Axiom 2): a reference copy is needed for comparison and repair, and actual DNA repair fixes a damaged strand using the complementary strand as reference. Helix = 1D information wound into 3D at a uniform angle at minimal cost (Axiom 3, Axiom 4); single-handed winding = CAS irreversibility (Axiom 2). Enzyme affinity = a continuous angle-match value ($\cos\theta$), not a discrete 0/1 = OPERATOR continuity (Axiom 3).

Discarded (not caught, cut without mercy): replication error rate (${\sim}10^{-9}$, an evolutionary compromise), 20 amino acids (not a framework integer; $4\times5$ would be numerology), helix 10.5 bp/turn (a mere approximation that cannot explain the 0.25 residue), major/minor groove ratio. In particular "2 strands = two brackets" is discarded — the framework's two brackets are orthogonal (independent), whereas DNA's two strands are complementary (dependent); the properties are opposite. Matching them just because both equal 2 is numerology.
