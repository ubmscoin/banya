D-81: $\rho(770) = \Lambda_{QCD} \times 7/2 = 777$ MeV — S-grade

$$m_\rho = \Lambda_{QCD} \times \frac{7}{2} = 777 \;\text{MeV}$$

Error 0.22% · Grade S

What: derives the $\rho(770)$ vector-meson mass of 777 MeV from the CAS state count.

Banya formula: $m_\rho = \Lambda_{QCD} \times 7/2$. The 7 is the total state count of the CAS operator (Axiom 2: the $2^3 - 1 = 7$ effective states of Read·Compare·Swap); the 2 is the minimum unit configuration of the d-ring that holds (juida) a quark-antiquark pair.

Substitution: $\Lambda_{QCD} = 222$ MeV (D-03). $222 \times 7/2 = 777$ MeV.

Axiom chain: Axiom 2 (CAS state count 7) ==> Axiom 3 (CAS 3-stage R·C·S) ==> Axiom 15 (d-ring ring buffer). The 7/2 ratio divides the full CAS state count by the 2-body meson structure.

Derivation: CAS Read (R+1) reads the quark flavor, Compare (C+1) checks it against the antiquark, and Swap (S+1) fixes the bound state. The vector meson has spin 1, so the utterance bit (Axiom 15, δ bit-7) sits ON and the full CAS state is active.

Numerics: computed value 777 MeV, experimental value 775.3 MeV.

Error: 0.22%. 0 free parameters. Only the CAS state count and $\Lambda_{QCD}$ are used.

Physical correspondence: $\rho$ meson is the dominant resonance in light-pair annihilation. In Banya, the quark-antiquark juim on the d-ring is a dense bound state that fully occupies all 7 CAS states.

Cross-check: in D-82 ($\omega$), adding the isospin-breaking correction $3(m_d - m_u)$ yields the $\omega(782)$ mass. The $\rho$-$\omega$ mass splitting is natural within the Banya structure.

Re-substitution: $m_\rho$ is the foundational scale for D-82 ($\omega$ meson) and for the vector-meson dominance (VMD) model. It is the CAS unit benchmark of the QCD binding energy.

Step 1. Banya Formula

The $\rho$ meson mass is the cost of a dense binding where the quark-antiquark juim on the d-ring occupies all CAS states. Since a vector meson has spin 1, the firing bit (Axiom 15 δ bit-7) is ON and all $2^3-1=7$ valid states (Axiom 2) of the CAS Read·Compare·Swap 3 axes (Axiom 3) are activated.

Step 2. Norm Substitution

The norm being substituted is the CAS state-count norm. It is chosen because $7/2$ — the total valid CAS states 7 (Axiom 2) divided by the quark-antiquark 2-body meson configuration — sets the binding scale, and the mass is read by multiplying this ratio by $\Lambda_{QCD}$.

$m_\rho = \Lambda_{QCD}\times\dfrac{N_{\text{CAS}}}{N_{\text{body}}}$
$m_\rho = \Lambda_{QCD}\times\dfrac{7}{2}$
$m_\rho$: $\rho(770)$ vector-meson mass | $7$: CAS valid states $2^3-1$ | $2$: meson 2-body configuration | $\Lambda_{QCD}$: QCD scale (D-03)

Step 4. Domain Transformation

The transformation is into the strong-force ring-7 color domain. What it reveals is the binding mass $m_\rho$ of the $\rho$ meson, the dominant resonance in lepton-pair annihilation. Multiplying the CAS state-count ratio $7/2$ by the QCD scale yields that value.

$m_\rho = 222\times\dfrac{7}{2} = 777\;\text{MeV}$
