Hypothesis -- H=0 is energy as a gauge freedom, equivalent to the Banya cost-conservation proposition (the firing period is not scheduled). No external clock = lossless cycling

A reparametrization-invariant theory has its canonical Hamiltonian vanish identically ($H\approx0$). The flow of time is the unfolding of a gauge transformation, with no external clock. $H=0$ means not that the universe's energy is zero but that energy is a gauge freedom. $H\approx0$ is itself the implicit form after now-fixing and transposing every term to one side, and time being a gauge means the labels of the contours that implicit form defines can be freely relabeled -- that is, the very time axis dropped into the domain by the level-set projection of D-157 reappears here as the gauge freedom.

The Axiom 15 cost-conservation proposition "the reason cost is conserved: the firing period of $\delta$ is not scheduled" writes the same statement from the opposite direction -- the firing period must be unscheduled (no external clock) so that internal cost increase/decrease is equivalently traded for firing-interval increase/decrease, conserving the total. Noether's theorem says time-translation invariance conserves energy, and in a fully parametrized theory that limit makes $H$ itself 0. The axiom statement that lossless cycling is possible only without an external clock and the standard result that the reparametrization gauge makes $H$ vanish are two faces of the same equivalence. Just as the freezing of WDW is no defect, $H\approx0$ is no defect but the structural expression of cost conservation. Even the distinction of system time (gauge parameter) and domain time (proper time, log) (D-143) parallels the standard coordinate-time/proper-time distinction. Free choice of the lapse $N$ (H-973) = non-scheduling of the firing interval.
