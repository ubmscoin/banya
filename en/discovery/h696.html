Polarization is the cost of restricting the oscillation direction of the CAS cost wave to one of the domain 4 axes (Axiom 1). Which axis the cost-wave oscillation (Axiom 4) occupies sets the polarization state.

Step 2. Norm Substitution

The norm being substituted is the transverse-oscillation norm of the domain 4 axes. Light can oscillate only in the 2 axes orthogonal to its propagation (the 4 axes of Axiom 1 minus the propagation axis), so the polarization DOF is 2. This 2-axis norm is chosen because it sets the polarization state, and the polarizer-transmission cost is substituted by Malus's law.

$\mathbf{E} = E_0\hat{\epsilon}\,e^{i(kz - \omega t)}$
$I = I_0\cos^2\theta$
