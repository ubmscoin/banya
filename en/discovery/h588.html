Step 2. Norm Substitution

The norm being substituted is the RLU decay-rate norm. It is chosen because the central non-equilibrium quantity, the entropy-production rate, is precisely the RLU non-stationary decay rate. In the stationary state the decay rate is 0 (equilibrium); in the transient state it is positive and converges to 0 as time proceeds.

$\dot{S} = -\dfrac{dN_{\text{excess}}}{dt} \ge 0$
$\dot{S}_{\text{eq}} = 0, \quad \dot{S}_{\text{noneq}} > 0$
$\dot{S}$: entropy-production rate = RLU non-stationary decay rate | $N_{\text{excess}}$: slots occupied above equilibrium | eq: stationary (equilibrium) state | noneq: non-stationary (non-equilibrium) transient state
