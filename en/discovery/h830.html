5-Step Recursive Substitution: Deriving Edge-of-Chaos Criticality from CAS Nonlinear Cost Coupling

Step 1. Banya Formula

$$\delta^2 = (\text{time} + \text{space})^2 + (\text{observer} + \text{superposition})^2$$

The cost of many CAS couples nonlinearly through pair crossing terms rather than as a simple sum (Axiom 4, H-826). Reading whether this nonlinear coupling diverges or decays through the cost term of the Banya formula, the separation rate of the cost trajectory over time is the maximal Lyapunov exponent $\lambda_{\max}$.

Step 2. Norm Substitution

The norm being substituted is the cost-trajectory separation-rate norm ($\lambda_{\max}$). When the nonlinear coupling is weak, cost decays to a fixed point ($\lambda_{\max}<0$, order); when strong, it diverges ($\lambda_{\max}>0$, chaos); and at the boundary between them, where there is neither divergence nor decay ($\lambda_{\max}\to 0^+$), criticality holds maximal computation — to reveal the information-processing maximum this norm is chosen.

$\lambda_{\max} = \lim_{t\to\infty}\dfrac{1}{t}\ln\dfrac{|\delta C(t)|}{|\delta C(0)|}$
$\lambda_{\max}<0$: order, $\;\;\lambda_{\max}>0$: chaos, $\;\;\lambda_{\max}\to 0^+$: criticality
$\lambda_{\max}$: maximal Lyapunov exponent | $\delta C(t)$: cost-trajectory perturbation | $t$: cycle count | criticality: cost neither diverges nor decays (maximal computation)

Step 3. Constant Substitution

lambda < 0   order     CAS cost decays -> fixed point
lambda > 0   chaos     CAS cost diverges
lambda = 0   critical  edge of chaos (maximal computation)
obs: lambda_max ~ 0   brain critical state

Step 4. Domain Transformation

The transformation is into the nonlinear-dynamics domain. What it reveals is the cost-coupling strength at which computation is maximized. Sweeping the nonlinearity from order to chaos yields the critical point where $\lambda_{\max}$ crosses 0.

$\dfrac{\partial \lambda_{\max}}{\partial(\text{coupling strength})}>0 \;\Rightarrow\; \exists\,\lambda_{\max}=0^+ \text{ (critical)}$

Step 5. Discovery

Measured: brain critical state $\lambda_{\max}\approx 0$
Result: CAS nonlinear criticality and brain criticality qualitatively match
