P-28: No Sterile Neutrino Above eV Scale

$$\text{CAS}=3\text{ steps (Read, Compare, Swap)},\ \text{no fourth}\ \Rightarrow\ \text{complete with 3 active neutrinos, sterile absence}$$

Prediction: a sterile neutrino cannot exist (CAS 3 steps = 3 generations, complete). Test: MicroBooNE/SBND (2026~).

In the Banya formula $\delta^2=(\text{time}+\text{space})^2+(\text{observer}+\text{superposition})^2$, particle generations correspond to the steps by which CAS processes a juim (Axiom 2).

CAS has exactly three steps — Read, Compare, Swap — and no fourth step. So the number of generations is complete at 3 (P-03).

The active neutrinos ($\nu_e,\nu_\mu,\nu_\tau$) map 1:1 onto these three steps and are exactly three.

A sterile neutrino is proposed as a fourth (anomalous) neutrino that does not undergo the standard weak interaction. But CAS has no fourth step to correspond to ==> an eV-scale sterile neutrino is structurally impossible.

A heavy keV-scale sterile neutrino (proposed as a dark-matter candidate) is likewise excluded — a particle outside the CAS 3 steps has no DOF to exist.

Dark matter is explained as ordinary juim in the RLU WARM state, not a new particle (P-20), so a sterile-neutrino dark matter is also unnecessary.

So this prediction is not "not found yet" but the structural-absence claim "the CAS 3-step structure has no fourth-neutrino DOF."

Indeed, the anomaly reported by MiniBooNE was not reproduced in the follow-up MicroBooNE, consistent with this prediction.
