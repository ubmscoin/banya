When this surplus cost exceeds the surface gravitational cost (Axiom 11), matter escapes; the comparison of the two costs is read through the irreversible-cost classification (Axiom 4).

The norm being substituted is the difference norm between the RLU surplus cost and the gravitational cost. It is chosen because the radiative momentum transferred by RLU decay is amplified by line opacity, driving the wind to a terminal velocity $v_\infty$ above the escape velocity $v_\text{esc}$. The mass-loss rate is therefore substituted by the surplus-cost emission rate. Why this norm: the Stellar-Wind Mass-Loss Rate itself originates from the RLU Surplus Cost, so only a norm that carries that very cost structure maps onto it without slack. Why the substitution is valid: the standard physics form on the left and the Banya form on the right denote the same quantity, the latter merely rewriting it in CAS cost terms.
