Supersymmetry (SUSY) was introduced to solve two problems — gauge-coupling unification and the hierarchy problem (stabilizing the Higgs mass).

The Banya framework solves coupling unification through CAS cost derivation and the hierarchy problem through the natural cutoff of the discrete d-ring structure ==> both are solved, so SUSY is unnecessary.
