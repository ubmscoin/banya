H-368: Neutrino mass m_νe = m_e × 7 × α³ = 1.39e-3 eV

$$m_{\nu_e}=m_e\times C(7,1)\times\alpha^3=1.39\times10^{-3}\;\text{eV}$$

(1) The electron-neutrino mass m_νe is the electron mass m_e multiplied by C(7,1) = 7 and by α³, giving 1.39 × 10⁻³ eV.

(2) Banya formula: m_νe = m_e × C(7,1) × α³ = 1.39 × 10⁻³ eV. C(7,1) = 7 is the single-DOF selection, and α³ is the 3-stage Compare suppression of CAS.

(3) By Axiom 9 (Pascal), C(7,1) = 7 is the number of ways to choose 1 out of 7 DOF. α is the Compare probability (D-01), and the cube corresponds to suppression at each of the three CAS stages (R, C, S).

(4) Structural consequence: a neutrino is a state in which all three CAS stages of the electron cycle are Compare-suppressed (α). Since only 1 of the 7 DOF is active, C(7,1) is multiplied. The juim is extremely small.

(5) Numerical values: m_e = 0.511 MeV = 511000 eV. α = 1/137.036. α³ = 3.88 × 10⁻⁷. m_νe = 511000 × 7 × 3.88 × 10⁻⁷ = 1.39 × 10⁻³ eV = 1.39 meV. Experimental upper bound < 0.8 eV, cosmological sum < 0.12 eV.

(6) Consistency: 1.39 meV lies within current experimental upper bounds. It is about 1/43 of the neutrino mass sum (H-369), so it is consistent with normal ordering.

(7) Physical correspondence: the neutrino mass generation mechanism (seesaw mechanism, Majorana mass) is the physical realization of the 3-stage CAS suppression. α³ corresponds to the seesaw ratio.

(8) If the mass ratios of the three neutrino generations are determined by CAS structural integers, then m_νμ/m_νe and m_ντ/m_νe can be predicted. H-406 proposes m_ν3/m_ν1 = √30.

(9) Verification: confirm whether values near 1.39 meV are measured in the KATRIN experiment (direct m_νe measurement) or next-generation neutrino mass experiments.
