Step 2. Norm Substitution

The norm being substituted is the $\alpha$-power ladder norm. This norm is chosen because the length ratio of a micro rung to a macro rung is set by an integer power of $\alpha$, and that integer rung count closes the scale hierarchy directly. $\dfrac{R_H}{\ell_P}\sim\alpha^{-n}$
$n=\dfrac{\ln(R_H/\ell_P)}{\ln(1/\alpha)}\approx 29$
