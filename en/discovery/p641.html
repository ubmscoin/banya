P-641. Neutrinos are Majorana: no lock = reversible = real class, $m_{\beta\beta} = 1.47 \sim 3.67$ meV

Key finding

P-641: the neutrino is a Majorana particle, its own antiparticle, and the effective mass of neutrinoless double beta decay has a floor that does not cancel to zero

$$\text{Compare skip} = \text{no lock} \Rightarrow \text{no order} \Rightarrow \text{reversible} \Rightarrow \text{real class} \Rightarrow \nu = \bar\nu, \qquad m_{\beta\beta} = \big|\,s_{12}^2 c_{13}^2\, m_2\, e^{i\phi} + s_{13}^2\, m_3\,\big| \in [1.47,\ 3.67]\ \text{meV}$$

Prediction (pending) -- not detected by next-generation ton-scale experiments (sensitivity about 10 meV), detected at the 1 meV scale. Resolves the library's internal contradiction (P-167 Dirac vs H-550, H-797, P-357 Majorana) with the level-spacing class proposition

The neutrino is the particle that skips Compare (H-05), whose text reads "Compare skip = no lock acquired". The lock is the device that enforces order: Axiom 5 "when the lock enforces order, simultaneity breaks and access becomes sequential; sequential access crosses +, so cost arises." Without the lock, order is not enforced. Without order there is no irreversibility: Axiom 4 "axes without order (observer, superposition) have no cost, no irreversibility and no discrete steps; irreversibility and discreteness are not separate -- they arise together from one cause, order." The operation defining the neutrino is therefore reversible, and by the level-spacing class proposition of Axiom 4 it is of real class, returning to its place (+1) after two reversals. The real class is the class that returns to itself under conjugation; its physical correspondence is particle = antiparticle, the Majorana condition.

An independent path reaches the same conclusion. The library holds that neutrinos are left-handed only (H-31, H-05) and that there are no light sterile (right-handed) neutrinos (P-28, P-165). A Dirac mass needs a left-handed component paired with a right-handed partner, so without that partner a nonzero neutrino mass must be a Majorana mass written with the left-handed component alone. Even under the seesaw reading that allows heavy right-handed components (P-489), the light neutrinos are Majorana. Either way, Dirac does not survive.

Re-substitution use: retires and replaces P-167 (Dirac prediction) as internally inconsistent. Closes from the axiom side the Majorana premise of H-550, H-797, P-357. The $m_{ee}\sim0.1$ meV of P-47 is incompatible with $m_1 = 0$ and normal ordering and is corrected to the range of this card.

Awaiting verification: Majorana nature is settled only by detecting neutrinoless double beta decay. The range 1.47-3.67 meV lies below the current KamLAND-Zen limit (28-122 meV) and next-generation ton-scale sensitivity, so the near-term verdict is "not detected at the next generation" and the final verdict comes from 1 meV-class experiments. Discarded if a falsification condition is met.
