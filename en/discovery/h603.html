H-603: Holevo Bound = 1 Classical Bit Extractable from 1 CAS = 1

$$\chi = S(\rho) - \sum_i p_i S(\rho_i) \;\geq\; I(X;Y),\quad \text{1 qubit} \to \text{at most 1 classical bit}$$

Grade: A

The Holevo bound states that the classical information extractable from 1 CAS operation (1 qubit measurement) is at most 1 bit. The Swap result of a d-ring 1-bit is either 0 or 1, i.e., 1 classical bit.

Axiom 2 (Swap = measurement = 1-bit output), Axiom 3 (d-ring 1 bit)

Axiom 2 (Swap output = 0 or 1 = 1 bit), Axiom 3 (d-ring discrete), Axiom 4 (cost +1 = 1 bit of information extracted)

Superluminal communication impossible (entanglement alone cannot transmit information). Superdense coding achieves 2 classical bits per qubit but requires a classical channel. 1 CAS = 1 bit is the absolute limit.

Classical bits extractable from 1 qubit = 1. Superdense coding = 2 (classical channel included).

The Holevo bound is an established result of quantum information theory.

Holevo bound (Holevo 1973), superdense coding, quantum information extraction.

Refuted if more than 2 classical bits are extracted from 1 qubit (without a classical channel).
