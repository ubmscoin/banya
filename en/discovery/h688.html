H-688: Galaxy Mergers = Cost Rearrangement of an ECS Entity Set

$$t_\text{merge} \sim t_\text{dyn}\,\frac{M_1+M_2}{\ln\Lambda\,M_2} \;\leftrightarrow\; \text{ECS cost-rearrangement time}$$

Grade: C

A galaxy merger is the gravitational binding and coalescence of two galaxies. In Banya, the ECS (Axiom 2) entity set (stars, gas, dark matter) relaxes via cost rearrangement (Axiom 4) into a new minimum-cost configuration. Dynamical friction = cost drag from trailing matter.

Axiom 2 (ECS entity set), Axiom 4 (cost rearrangement), H-687 (dark-matter halo)

Axiom 2 (ECS = arrangement of stars+gas+DM within a galaxy), Axiom 4 (cost minimisation ==> equilibrium of the merger product), Axiom 11 (gravitational cost = dynamical friction, Chandrasekhar formula). Major merger ($M_1 \sim M_2$): morphological transformation. Minor merger ($M_2 \ll M_1$): absorption.

Major merger ==> elliptical-galaxy formation. Tidal tails (NGC 4038/4039 Antennae galaxies). Merger-induced starburst. Central black-hole merger ==> gravitational waves (LISA band). Milky Way -- Andromeda merger: in $\sim 4.5\;\text{Gyr}$.

Consistent with N-body simulations (Illustris, FIRE) and observed merger rates to $< 50\%$.

5-Step Recursive Substitution: Deriving the Galaxy-Merger Timescale from ECS Cost Rearrangement

Step 1. Banya Formula

$$\delta^2 = (\text{time} + \text{space})^2 + (\text{observer} + \text{superposition})^2$$

A galaxy merger is the process in which two ECS (Axiom 2) entity sets (stars, gas, dark matter) relax into a lower-cost arrangement. Axiom 4 (cost minimization) sets the equilibrium, and Axiom 11 (gravitational cost = dynamical friction) sets the relaxation time.

Step 2. Norm Substitution

(a) The norm being substituted is the dynamical-friction norm (Chandrasekhar) of Axiom 11. (b) It is chosen because the cost drag the trailing matter exerts on the secondary galaxy sets the merger rate. The merger time is substituted by the dynamical time $t_\text{dyn}$ scaled by the mass ratio and the Coulomb logarithm.

$t_\text{merge} \sim t_\text{dyn}\,\dfrac{M_1+M_2}{\ln\Lambda\;M_2}$
$t_\text{merge}$: merger time | $t_\text{dyn}$: dynamical time | $M_1$: primary mass | $M_2$: secondary mass | $\ln\Lambda$: Coulomb logarithm

Step 3. Constant Substitution

$t_{dyn}$ = 0.5 $\text{Gyr}$ Milky-Way scale
$M_{1}$ = $1\times10^{12}$ $M_{sun}$ Milky Way
$M_{2}$ = $1\times10^{12}$ $M_{sun}$ M31 (major merger)
$\ln$ L = 3 Coulomb logarithm

Step 4. Domain Transformation

(a) The transformation is into the cosmological timescale domain. (b) What it reveals is the time for the ECS cost rearrangement to complete. Multiplying the dynamical time by the mass-ratio and Coulomb-log factors yields the merger time.

$t_\text{merge} \sim 0.5\times\dfrac{2\times10^{12}}{3\times10^{12}} \approx 0.33\;\text{Gyr per orbit}$
cumulative infall $\sim 4\text{--}5\;\text{Gyr}$ (multiple close passages)

Step 5. Discovery

Derived: Milky Way-M31 merger $\sim 4.5\;\text{Gyr}$
Measured: $\sim 4.5\;\text{Gyr}$ (van der Marel 2012)
Agreement: N-body simulations (Illustris, FIRE) $< 50\%$
