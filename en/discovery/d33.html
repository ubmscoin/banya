The Fermi degeneracy-pressure exponent 5/3 is the pressure ratio produced by the degrees of freedom not occupied by Swap when juim fills the d-ring. It is read as the ratio that distributes, over the CAS 3 stages (Axiom 2), the freedom remaining after subtracting the domain 4 bits (Axiom 1) that Swap occupies from the complete-description 9 bits (Axiom 9).

Step 2. Norm Substitution

The norm being substituted is the non-Swap DOF norm. The degeneracy pressure is the pressure made by the residual freedom that cannot be Swapped, and dividing that residual by the number of CAS stages sets the equation-of-state exponent — so this norm is chosen. The exponent 5/3 is therefore substituted by the ratio of the 5 left after subtracting the Swap-occupied 4 from the complete-description 9, over the CAS 3 stages.

$P \propto (N/V)^{\gamma}$
$\gamma = \dfrac{5}{3} = \dfrac{9 - 4}{3}$
$\gamma$: degeneracy-pressure exponent | $9$: complete-description bits (Axiom 9) | $4$: Swap-occupied domain bits (Axiom 1) | $5=9-4$: residual non-Swap DOF | $3$: CAS stages (Axiom 2)

Step 4. Domain Transformation

The transformation is into the non-relativistic Fermi-gas equation-of-state domain. What it reveals is the density exponent $\gamma$ of the pressure made by the non-Swap DOF. Dividing the residual freedom 5 by the CAS 3 stages yields that value.
