Core Finding

H-349: Relativity of simultaneity = different screen renderings of the same system tick

$$\text{same }t_{\text{system}}\;\to\;\text{different }t_{\text{domain}}\text{ per entity (ECS local)}$$

(1) The relativity of simultaneity is the phenomenon where the same system tick renders differently on the screens of different entities. Each entity runs locally in its own ECS.

(2) Banya formula: same t_system ==> different t_domain per entity (ECS local). System time is global, but domain time is local per entity.

(3) By Axiom 12 (ECS), each entity has an independent set of components. By Axiom 3 (screen), rendering is based on local DATA. By Axiom 11 (d-ring), entity-to-entity interaction goes through the ring seam.

(4) Structural consequence: two events that are "simultaneous" on entity A's screen may be rendered as "sequential" on entity B's screen. This is because the CAS Swap costs of A and B are different.

(5) Numerical values: if the Swap cost ratio of the two entities is γ = 1/√(1−v²/c²), then 1 unit of A's domain time corresponds to γ units of B's domain time.

(6) Consistency: matches exactly with the relativity of simultaneity in special relativity. The Lorentz transformation of Minkowski spacetime translates into a per-entity screen rendering transformation.

(7) Physical correspondence: Lorentz contraction and time dilation are the rendering differences in the space domain and time domain respectively. The two effects are coupled and guarantee the invariance of the speed of light (H-351).

(8) In existing physics, the relativity of simultaneity is assumed in principle (Einstein's two postulates). In the Banya Framework it is derived from the locality of ECS and the cost dependence of screen rendering.

(9) Verification: from the Swap cost difference between two entities A and B, construct the Lorentz transformation matrix, and confirm that the time dilation/length contraction formulas of special relativity are reproduced.
