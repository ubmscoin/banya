(3) By Axiom 3 (screen), DATA is the rendering result. By Axiom 15, system time is defined as the count of δ utterances. Domain time (bit 2) is the value rendered on the screen.

(4) Structural consequence: how much domain time progresses during 1 system tick depends on the CAS Swap cost of that entity. The greater the cost (the stronger the juim), the smaller the progression of domain time.

(7) Physical correspondence: time dilation is an increase in CAS Swap cost ==> decrease in domain time rendering. Time slowing near a black hole is the CAS Swap cost being maximized (H-348).

(8) The common notion that "Planck time is the minimum time" is a statement from the screen perspective. From the system perspective, 1 tick is 1 tick, and whatever it appears as on the screen, at the system level it is an identical 1 CAS cycle.
