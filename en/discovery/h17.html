Core Finding

H-17: Mapping CAS to G_SM: principal bundle projection

Map CAS (OPERATOR) to the total space of a principal bundle, and DATA (spacetime) to the base space. Writing (juigi) = projection. A gauge transformation means that CAS can write the same DATA via different internal paths.

In δ² = (t+s)² + (o+sp)², (t+s) is the base space (spacetime) and (o+sp) is the fiber (internal space). CAS is an operation on the fiber, and writing (juigi) is the projection from fiber to base space (π: P ==> M).

Axiom 1 (Banya formula: separation of DATA and OPERATOR) ==> Axiom 2 (CAS sole operator) ==> Axiom 11 (multiple projection). The multiple projection of Axiom 11 corresponds to a section of the principal bundle. Gauge transformation = freedom of section choice.

Because it is a projection rather than an isomorphism, the remaining task "CAS is not a group" (H-02 remaining task) is resolved. CAS is not what acts as a group; it is the space on which a group acts. The structure group on the fiber is G_SM = U(1)×SU(2)×SU(3).

Connection = the path by which CAS accesses DATA. Curvature = non-commutativity of the connection = field strength. The Yang-Mills equations could emerge from a path-variation principle on the CAS workbench.

The principal bundle structure is the standard mathematical language of gauge theory. If the CAS-DATA structure is isomorphic to a principal bundle, then the entire gauge theory is reproduced from CAS.

Modern gauge theory (Yang-Mills) is a theory of connections on a principal bundle. The Banya Framework identifies the total space of the principal bundle as CAS and the base space as DATA.

The Standard Model assumes the principal bundle structure and develops physics on top of it. The Banya Framework claims that the principal bundle structure follows necessarily from CAS-DATA separation (Axiom 1).

It requires a mathematical proof that the CAS-DATA mapping satisfies all properties of a principal bundle (local triviality, transition functions, etc.). If satisfied, it is a discovery; otherwise it remains an analogy.

Identify what the topological properties of the principal bundle (Chern classes, etc.) correspond to in the CAS structure. Examine whether instantons correspond to non-trivial topological structures of CAS.
