$f_\pi = \Lambda_{\text{QCD}}/\sqrt{3} = 128.2\;\text{MeV}$. $\sqrt{3}$ = geometric mean of CAS 3 stages (Read, Compare, Swap). $\Lambda_{\text{QCD}}$ is the CAS strong-coupling scale.

Axiom 3 (CAS 3-stage) yields the $\sqrt{3}$. D-03 ($\alpha_s$) determines $\Lambda_{\text{QCD}}$. The energy scale where CAS places juim is $f_\pi$.

$f_\pi$ is the d-ring oscillation amplitude of a quark-antiquark pair under juim in the CAS strong-coupling regime. Dividing by $\sqrt{3}$ is the geometric reduction of the phase space contributed by each of the CAS 3 stages.
