Bose-Einstein condensation is the phenomenon in which many boson entities crowd into the same FSM ground state. The FSM ground is the 000 state of minimal norm (Axiom 9, Axiom 11), and because CAS Swap is non-exclusive (Axiom 4), multiple occupancy of one state is allowed.

Step 2. Norm Substitution

The norm being substituted is the FSM ground norm. The condensation target is the 000 state of minimal norm, and bosons can stack into that single state — which is why the norm is chosen. The occupancy number $N_0$ is substituted by the count of entities crowded into the same FSM ground, and non-exclusive Swap permits that macroscopic occupancy.

$\|N\|_\text{FSM} = 0 \;\text{ (000 ground)},\qquad \text{Swap non-exclusive} \Rightarrow N_0 \gg 1$
$T < T_c \;\Rightarrow\; \dfrac{N_0}{N} \to 1$
$\|N\|_\text{FSM}$: FSM norm (Axiom 11) | 000: ground state (Axiom 9) | $N_0$: ground occupancy | $T_c$: BEC transition temperature (set by norm and density)

Step 4. Domain Transformation

$T < T_c \;\Rightarrow\; N_0 / N \to 1 \;\text{ (macroscopic quantum state)}$
