H-635: Giant Magnetoresistance = Cost Conduction Change by Lock Bit Alignment

$$\text{GMR} = \frac{R_{\uparrow\downarrow} - R_{\uparrow\uparrow}}{R_{\uparrow\uparrow}} \;\leftrightarrow\; \text{lock-bit parallel/antiparallel CAS cost-conduction difference}$$

Giant magnetoresistance (GMR) is the phenomenon whereby lock-bit alignment in magnetic layers drastically changes CAS cost conduction. Parallel alignment = low scattering = low resistance. Antiparallel = high scattering = high resistance.

GMR is therefore substituted by the relative difference of parallel and antiparallel resistance. Why this norm: Fe/Cr GMR $\sim 80\%$ (4.2\,K) itself originates from the lock-bit-alignment CAS cost conduction, so only a norm that carries that very cost structure maps onto it without slack.
