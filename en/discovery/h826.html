Core Finding

H-826: Complex-System Emergence = Nonlinear Collective Effect of Many CAS

$$\text{Emergence} \;\leftrightarrow\; \sum_i \text{CAS}_i \neq N \cdot \text{CAS}_1 \;\text{(nonlinear cost coupling)}$$

Grade: B

Emergence is the phenomenon in which properties absent from individual constituents appear at the collective level. In Banya, when many CAS couple nonlinearly, the total cost is not the simple sum of individual costs but forms a new macroscopic cost structure. This is emergence.

Axiom 2 (many CAS), Axiom 4 (nonlinear cost coupling)

Axiom 2 (CAS = sole operator), Axiom 4 (cost +1 = individual cost). When many CAS operate simultaneously, the crossing cost (Axiom 4) couples nonlinearly. $\text{Cost}(\{CAS_i\}) \neq \sum \text{Cost}(CAS_i)$. This difference is the cost of the emergent phenomenon.

Consciousness (H-835) = emergence of δ utterance. Life (H-817) = emergence of the CAS loop. Society = emergence of many observers. Because emergence is the universal result of nonlinear CAS coupling, it appears at every scale. Irreducibility = nonlinear cost cannot be decomposed into individual costs.

Emergence indicator: mutual information $I > \sum H_i - H_{\text{joint}}$.

Consistent with complex-systems theory.
