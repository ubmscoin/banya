Step 2. Norm Substitution

(a) The norm being substituted is the FSM asymmetry norm of the cost sum $B(Z)$ at fixed mass number $A$. (b) It is chosen because the stable proton number falls directly out of the cost-minimum condition $\partial B/\partial Z = 0$ (Axiom 4). Adding the CAS electromagnetic cost $a_C Z^2 A^{-1/3}$ and the FSM asymmetry cost $a_A(A-2Z)^2/A$ and differentiating by $Z$ gives the stability line $Z_0(A)$. Why this norm: the Stable Proton Number $Z_0$ itself originates from the CAS Electromagnetic Cost, so only a norm that carries that very cost structure maps onto it without slack. Why the substitution is valid: the standard physics form on the left and the Banya form on the right denote the same quantity, the latter merely rewriting it in CAS cost terms.

$Z_0 = \dfrac{A}{2}\dfrac{1}{1 + A^{2/3} a_C/(4a_A)}$
