D-163. Symmetry class $\beta = 2$ of Riemann-zero statistics = complex class of irreversible CAS (level-spacing class proposition)

Key finding

D-163: the random-matrix class followed by Riemann-zero spacings is the complex class of Dyson's three (GUE, $\beta=2$); Banya obtains it from CAS irreversibility (no time reversal) and the complex form of the 2-axis OPERATOR bracket

$$R_2(u) = 1 - \left(\frac{\sin\pi u}{\pi u}\right)^{2}, \qquad p(s) \propto s^{\beta}\ (s\to0), \qquad \beta = 2$$

Discovery, grade B -- level-repulsion exponent $\beta = 2$, 0% error (Montgomery 1973, Odlyzko 1987). Uses only 1, 2, 3. Retrospective reconstruction of a known fact

In Dyson's threefold way the eigenvalue statistics of Hermitian operators fall into exactly three classes: real symmetric (GOE, $\beta=1$, time-reversal symmetry with $T^2=+1$), complex Hermitian (GUE, $\beta=2$, no time-reversal symmetry), quaternion self-dual (GSE, $\beta=4$, $T^2=-1$). Montgomery's pair correlation $1-(\sin\pi u/\pi u)^2$ equals the GUE pair correlation, and Odlyzko confirmed it numerically with zeros near height $10^{12}$. The literature therefore holds that a Hilbert-Polya operator with the zeros as eigenvalues must break time-reversal symmetry.

In Banya this class is fixed by the level-spacing class proposition of Axiom 4: "the number system is fixed by the orthogonality of the Banya equation, and the level-spacing class is fixed by cost." On the number-system side, the Axiom 1 proposition (recursion principle) lists one orthogonal axis as the real line, two as unit-circle complex numbers and three as unit-sphere quaternions -- the same list as Dyson's $\mathbb R, \mathbb C, \mathbb H$. On the class side, irreversible CAS takes part in the operation generating the zeros. CAS transitions cannot be reversed (Axiom 2 proposition: R==>C==>S cannot run in reverse, no refund), so the sign of two reversals is undefined, and the real class (two reversals $+1$) and quaternion class (two reversals $-1$), both requiring a reverse, drop out together, leaving the complex class. The complex form of the two OPERATOR axes is the structural container; what fixes the class is irreversibility on the cost layer.

Re-substitution use: closes which statistics the frequencies of H-995 (zeros = domain-time frequencies) obey. Next-round test: whether the same rule holds for every quantum-chaotic spectrum involving irreversible CAS (systems with intact time reversal show GOE).

Scope of the discovery: the class decision (irreversible ==> $\beta=2$) is a direct application of axiom passages, but GUE statistics and Montgomery's formula are known facts. That the zeros are eigenvalues of an OPERATOR-bracket operator is a reading on top of the Hilbert-Polya hypothesis.
