5-Step Recursive Substitution: Deriving the Scale Hierarchy from the Compare-true Frequency

Step 1. Banya Formula

$$\delta^2 = (\text{time} + \text{space})^2 + (\text{observer} + \text{superposition})^2$$

The energy-scale hierarchy is created by the frequency at which Compare returns true (Axiom 7). That frequency is $\alpha$, and the cost-+1 unit (Axiom 4) counts one interaction at each scale. The enormous ratios between scales accumulate as powers of $\alpha$.

Step 2. Norm Substitution

The norm being substituted is the $\alpha$-power norm. (a) It is a logarithmic-scale norm with base the Compare-true frequency $\alpha$, and (b) it is chosen because each scale transition is where the CAS cost mode changes, so the inter-scale ratio closes in the form $\alpha^n$. From Planck down to QCD, each rung of one ladder is one power of $\alpha$.

$E_{\text{GUT}} = E_{\text{Planck}}\,\alpha^{n},\quad E_{\text{EW}} = E_{\text{GUT}}\,\alpha^{m},\quad E_{\text{QCD}} = E_{\text{EW}}\,\alpha^{k}$
$\log_{1/\alpha}\!\dfrac{E_{\text{Planck}}}{E_{\text{QCD}}} = n + m + k$
$\alpha$: Compare-true frequency = fine-structure constant (D-01) | $E_{\text{Planck}}, E_{\text{GUT}}, E_{\text{EW}}, E_{\text{QCD}}$: 4 scales | $n,m,k$: $\alpha$ exponent per sector

Step 4. Domain Transformation

The transformation is into the energy-scale domain. What it reveals is how many rungs of a base-$1/\alpha$ ladder span the whole ratio from Planck to QCD. Taking the log of the total ratio to base $1/\alpha$ counts the ladder rungs.

$\dfrac{E_{\text{Planck}}}{\Lambda_{\text{QCD}}} = \dfrac{1.22\times 10^{19}}{0.2} \approx 6.1\times 10^{19}$
$\log_{137.036}(6.1\times 10^{19}) \approx 9.2$
