Core Finding

H-632: Anderson Localization = Disorder-Driven Blockade of CAS Cost Propagation

$$|\psi(r)| \sim e^{-|r-r_0|/\xi} \;\leftrightarrow\; \text{exponential decay of CAS cost propagation}$$

Grade: B

Anderson localization is the phenomenon in which disorder on the ECS lattice (random cost potential) exponentially blocks CAS cost propagation. The localization length $\xi$ = the maximum distance over which CAS cost can propagate.

Axiom 7 (ECS disorder), Axiom 4 (cost propagation)

Axiom 7 (ECS lattice disorder = random cost), Axiom 4 (cost propagation blockade = localization), Axiom 6 (competition between RLU decay and disorder)

Mobility edge = CAS cost threshold. Metal-insulator transition = divergence/convergence of the localization length. 1D/2D are always localized (scaling theory). Only 3D admits a transition.

3D Anderson transition critical disorder $W_c/t \approx 16.5$ (lattice model).

Consistent with Anderson localization (1958) theory and experiment.

Anderson localization (1958), metal-insulator transition, scaling theory.

Direct observation of localization in cold-atom lattice experiments (2008).
