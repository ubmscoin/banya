P-33: $dw/da=0$ Exactly (Dark-energy Equation of State Invariant)

That the energy density is constant regardless of expansion is precisely the structural reason for the equation of state $w=-1$ (P-04). This is the identity of the cosmological constant.

Since COLD is irreversibly fixed, $w$ has no time (scale) dependence ==> in the CPL parameterization $w(a)=w_0+w_a(1-a)$, $w_a=0$ and $w_0=-1$.

So $dw/da=0$ is not an approximation but an exact consequence of RLU COLD irreversibility — dark energy does not change dynamically.

This is directly distinguishable from quintessence (a rolling scalar field) or phantom dark-energy models, which predict time variation with $w_a\neq0$.
