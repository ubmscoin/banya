P-18: QCD $b_0$ Numerator = CAS Ring-size Pattern

$$b_0=\frac{11N_c-2n_f}{3}\;\xrightarrow{n_f=6}\;\frac{33-12}{3}=7=\underbrace{4}_{\text{domain}}+\underbrace{3}_{\text{CAS}},\qquad n_f=3\Rightarrow b_0=9$$

Prediction: the QCD 1-loop $b_0$ numerator structure matches the CAS DOF (7, 9). Test: LHC reanalysis (immediate).

In the Banya formula $\delta^2=(\text{time}+\text{space})^2+(\text{observer}+\text{superposition})^2$, the running of a coupling is the question of how the cost CAS pays to move by one cell varies with scale (Axiom 2). The β-function coefficient must therefore reflect the CAS ring size.

The QCD 1-loop β-function coefficient is $b_0=(11N_c-2n_f)/3$. With six quark flavors ($n_f=6$), $b_0=(33-12)/3=7$ (D-44).

7 matches the CAS full-description DOF exactly -- the 4 domain axes (Axiom 1) plus the 3 CAS steps (Axiom 2): $4+3=7$ (Axiom 9).

With $n_f=3$ (three light quarks), $b_0=(33-6)/3=9$. 9 is the full-description 7 plus the 2 classical brackets (Axiom 9: $7+2=9$) -- the cell count of the d-ring.

So the gear ratio of QCD running equals the CAS ring size. Because the CAS ring has 7 cells and the d-ring has 9, $b_0$ moves between 7 and 9 with the number of active quarks.

This means $b_0$ is not an arbitrary combinatorial number but the cell count of the CAS structure ==> the difference of $11N_c$ and $2n_f$ lands exactly on axiom integers.
