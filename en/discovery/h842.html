H-842. Semiconductor = Doping Control of FSM-Norm Bandgap

Core Finding

H-842: Semiconductor = doping control of the FSM-norm bandgap

$$E_g = \|F\|_{\text{cond}} - \|F\|_{\text{val}} \;;\quad n_e \propto e^{-E_g / 2k_BT} \;\;\text{(doping }\to E_g\text{ tuning)}$$

Grade: B

A semiconductor is a crystal-lattice structure in which the FSM norm forms a bandgap $E_g$. The FSM-norm difference between the valence band and the conduction band is the bandgap, and doping inserts an external FSM into the lattice to create a new energy level inside that gap.

Axiom 4 proposition (FSM norm), Axiom 12 (ECS lattice), Axiom 2 (CAS Compare)

Axiom 3: the FSM norm determines the electron energy levels. Bandgap = forbidden interval between allowed FSM norms. Doping = insertion of an external FSM that creates donor/acceptor levels inside the forbidden interval. Axiom 2: the ECS lattice supplies a periodic potential. Axiom 5: CAS Compare mediates electron-hole pair creation and recombination.

n-type doping: donor level $E_d \approx E_c - 0.05\;\text{eV}$ ==> supplies electrons to the conduction band. p-type doping: acceptor level $E_a \approx E_v + 0.05\;\text{eV}$ ==> generates holes in the valence band. pn junction: depletion layer forms at the CAS boundary between the two doped regions.

Si bandgap: $1.12\;\text{eV}$. Ge: $0.67\;\text{eV}$. GaAs: $1.42\;\text{eV}$. Doping concentration: $10^{15}$~$10^{20}\;\text{cm}^{-3}$.

Si bandgap theory matches the measured value to $< 1\%$.

Semiconductor physics, band theory, doping, pn junction, solid-state physics.

Over 60 years of empirical validation by the semiconductor industry. Precision measurement of the Si, Ge, GaAs bandgaps.

Complete ab initio derivation of the band structure from the FSM norm.
