5-Step Recursive Substitution: Deriving the Critical Exponents $\nu, \gamma$ from CAS Cost Scaling

Step 1. Banya Formula

The universality of phase transitions states that the critical behavior of RLU decay (Axiom 6: RLU = temperature scale) depends only on the CAS structure, not on microscopic detail. The CAS structure is fixed by dimension and symmetry, and the 4 axes of Axiom 1 force both the 3 spatial dimensions and the upper critical dimension $d_c=4$.

Step 2. Norm Substitution

The norm being substituted is the CAS cost-scaling norm (Axiom 4: CAS cost = critical exponents). This norm is chosen because the critical exponents are fixed as the scaling dimension of the CAS cost near the renormalization-group fixed point under scale transformation. The divergences of specific heat, susceptibility, and correlation length are therefore substituted by CAS cost-scaling exponents.

$\xi \sim |T-T_c|^{-\nu},\quad \chi \sim |T-T_c|^{-\gamma}$
$\nu = \dfrac{1}{2} + \dfrac{1}{12}\,\varepsilon,\quad \gamma = 1 + \dfrac{1}{6}\,\varepsilon,\quad \varepsilon = d_c - d$
$\xi$: correlation length | $\chi$: susceptibility | $\nu, \gamma$: CAS cost-scaling critical exponents | $d_c=4$: upper critical dimension = 4 axes of Axiom 1 | $\varepsilon$: $4-d$ expansion parameter

Step 3. Constant Substitution

d_c     = 4                upper critical dimension = Axiom 1 four axes
d       = 3                3 spatial dimensions
epsilon = d_c - d = 1
nu_exp  = 0.630            3D Ising experimental value

Step 4. Domain Transformation

The transformation is into the 3D Ising universality-class domain. What it reveals is the set of critical exponents $\nu, \gamma$ fixed by CAS symmetry + dimension alone. Substituting $\varepsilon=1$ into the first-order expansion yields the value.

$\nu = \dfrac{1}{2} + \dfrac{1}{12} = 0.583,\quad \gamma = 1 + \dfrac{1}{6} = 1.167$
