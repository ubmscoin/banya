Core Finding

P-96: CLFV Branching Ratio $=\alpha^7\sim10^{-15}$

$$\text{BR}(\mu\to e\gamma)\sim\alpha^7\sim10^{-15}\quad(7=\text{완전기술자유도 전체 순회 비용})$$

Prediction: charged-lepton flavor violation $\text{BR}(\mu\to e\gamma)\sim\alpha^7\sim10^{-15}$. Test: MEG II (2028~).

In the Banya formula $\delta^2=(\text{time}+\text{space})^2+(\text{observer}+\text{superposition})^2$, charged-lepton flavor violation (CLFV, $\mu\to e\gamma$) is a transition from one generation index to another, requiring a full traversal of the full-description DOF 7 (Axiom 9).

A full 7-bit traversal passes the CAS selection cost $\alpha$ seven times, so the branching ratio is $\alpha^7\sim10^{-15}$.

$\mu\to e\gamma$ is the channel in which a muon flavor-violatingly decays to an electron and a photon, extremely suppressed in the Standard Model.

The Banya framework quantifies this suppression as $\alpha^7$ ==> the full-description DOF 7 enters the exponent directly.

$\alpha^7=(1/137)^7\sim10^{-15}$, two orders below the current MEG bound $4.2\times10^{-13}$.

So this prediction is not "CLFV is rare" but the quantitative claim "exactly $\alpha^7\sim10^{-15}$."

This is in the same $\alpha^n$ traversal-cost family as CPT violation $\alpha^8$ (P-59) and strong CP $\alpha^8$ (P-25), where $n$ is the number of traversed bits.

Many BSM models (SUSY, etc.) predict CLFV much larger (near current sensitivity), but the Banya framework suppresses it extremely to $\alpha^7$.

Test: if MEG II ($10^{-15}$ sensitivity, 2028~) confirms $\text{BR}(\mu\to e\gamma)\sim\alpha^7$, this prediction is supported.

Refutation: if CLFV is 0 or disagrees with $\alpha^7$ by more than $3\sigma$ (especially a larger value), this prediction is discarded.

Re-substitution purpose: Test: MEG II ($10^{-15}$ sensitivity, 2028~). Refutation: CLFV is 0 or disagrees with $\alpha^7$ by $3\sigma$. Source: Axiom 9 (full-description DOF 7), D-01 (α), Axiom 15 (8-bit ring).

5-Step Recursive Substitution: Deriving the CLFV Branching Ratio $\alpha^7$ from the Full-Description DOF 7 Traversal Cost

Step 1. Banya Formula

$$\delta^2 = (\text{time} + \text{space})^2 + (\text{observer} + \text{superposition})^2$$

Charged-lepton flavor violation $\mu\to e\gamma$ is a transition from one generation index to another — a cost that requires traversing the full-description DOF 7 (Axiom 9: domain 4 + CAS 3) in full. This transition occurs in the superposition domain.

Step 2. Norm Substitution

(a) The norm being substituted is the traversal-cost norm that visits each of the full-description DOF 7 once. (b) A generation-index crossing fails to close if any one of the 7 bits is skipped, so every bit pays the CAS Compare selection cost $\alpha$ (D-01). The branching ratio is therefore substituted by the 7th power of $\alpha$.

$\text{BR}(\mu\to e\gamma) = \displaystyle\prod_{k=1}^{7}\alpha = \alpha^{7}$
$\text{BR}(\mu\to e\gamma) \sim \alpha^{7} \sim 10^{-15}$
$\text{BR}$: branching ratio | $\alpha$: fine-structure constant = CAS Compare cost (D-01) | $7$: full-description DOF = domain 4 + CAS 3 (Axiom 9) | 7th power: full 7-bit traversal

Step 3. Constant Substitution

alpha   = 1/137.036          D-01
7       = 4 + 3              Axiom 9 (domain 4 + CAS 3)
alpha^7 = (1/137.036)^7
MEG     = 4.2e-13            current limit (mu -> e gamma)

Step 4. Domain Transformation

(a) The transformation is into the superposition domain (generation-index transition). (b) What it reveals is the branching ratio for the muon's flavor-violating decay into an electron and a photon. Multiplying $\alpha$ seven times yields that value.

$\alpha^{7} = \left(\dfrac{1}{137.036}\right)^{7} = 1.5\times10^{-15}$
