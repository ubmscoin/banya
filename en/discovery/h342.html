H-342. δ Indescribable Within FSM = Structural Source of Gödel Incompleteness

Core Finding

H-342: δ indescribable within FSM = structural source of Gödel incompleteness

$$\delta\notin\text{FSM}\;\Rightarrow\;\text{FSM cannot prove statements about }\delta$$

(1) δ is a global flag outside the FSM (Axiom 15), so the internal rules of the FSM (Axiom 1, Axiom 14) cannot prove statements about δ. This is the Banya Framework's translation of Gödel's incompleteness.

(2) Banya formula: δ ∉ FSM ⇒ FSM cannot prove statements about δ. Complete description of one's own equality within a formal system is impossible in principle.

(3) Axiom 15 defines δ as the unique global flag outside the FSM. The FSM (Axiom 14) cycles through the four states 000==>001==>011==>111, but δ itself is the external ignition condition for this cycle.

(4) Structural consequence: in Gödel's incompleteness theorem, "self-referential statements cannot be proved" is, in the Banya Framework, equivalent to the FSM being unable to describe its own ignition condition (δ) in its internal language.

(5) None of the 2⁷=128 effective states of the FSM can express δ itself. δ is the 8th bit (bit 7), the precondition for the remaining 7 bits.

(6) Consistency: the Gödel sentence G is "G is not provable in this system". In the Banya Framework, no CAS cycle inside the FSM can read "why δ uttered". The structure matches.

(7) Physical correspondence: in quantum mechanics, the measurement problem — that the cause of wave function collapse cannot be explained by the wave function itself — is the physical manifestation of this structure.

(8) Turing's halting problem has the same structure. For the FSM to predetermine whether its own δ will utter, it would have to read δ, but δ is not a target of CAS Read (Axiom 15).

(9) Verification: map an arbitrary formal system to an FSM, and identify the external condition corresponding to δ; the incompleteness theorem of that system should automatically be derived.

(10) Remaining task: correspondence with Tarski's undefinability theorem, and the δ-structure translation of second incompleteness (impossibility of self-consistency proof) are pending.
