Banya formula: $\sigma \approx (440\;\text{MeV})^2 \approx 0.18\;\text{GeV}^2$. The cost for CAS to maintain a juim (grip) state is linearly proportional to the distance (the number of d-ring shifts).

Numerics: prediction $0.18\;\text{GeV}^2$, experiment $0.18 \pm 0.02\;\text{GeV}^2$. Extracted from lattice QCD and the quarkonium spectrum. Agreement within the error bar.
