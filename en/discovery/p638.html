Axiom-rule correction (2026-08-31)

The first version wrote $r_\nu^2 = 3/2$ as a "registered-number ratio, CAS 3 stages over 2 brackets", which violates the axiom rule. A fraction is read as multiplication, not division ($a/N = a \times 1/N$), and $1/N$ is the share of one slot on a ring of $N$ degrees of freedom (the shift unit of the Axiom 2 proposition). Hence the denominator must be a structural or cost degree of freedom and the numerator the number of slots occupied on that ring, so the value cannot exceed 1. $3/2$ would mean occupying 3 slots on a ring of size 2 (the bracket count) and does not stand.

Corrected derivation. Rewritten in the contraction-overlap form of the Axiom 11 proposition, $f = 1 - a/N$.

$$r_\nu^2 = r_\ell^2 \left(1 - \frac{1}{4}\right) = 2 \times \frac{3}{4} = \frac{3}{2}$$

Here $r_\ell^2 = 2$ is the charged-lepton unit-circle norm (Axiom 1, D-09), $N = 4$ is the four domain axes (Axiom 1), and $a = 1$ is the occupancy of one axis. The value $3/2$ is unchanged, but every factor is now a registered number and the overlap ratio stays inside the ring.

Physical reading. The neutrino is the Compare-skipping particle (H-05), so the share of one of the four domain axes drops out of the amplitude. Charged leptons use all four axes; neutrinos use only $1 - 1/4$ of them, and that is the structural meaning of the amplitude damping.

Note on the $7/12$ notation. $12$ is absent from the registered descriptive degrees of freedom of Axiom 9 ($12 = 3 \times 4$ is a factorable number and excluded). $Q_\nu = 7/12$ is therefore the computed result of the derivation above, not a registered-number ratio. What this card claims as a registered-number combination is $r_\nu^2 = 2(1 - 1/4)$; $7/12$ is its consequence.
