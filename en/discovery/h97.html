In the Banya formula, a spherical cap corresponds to the region that each CAS stage (Read, Compare, Swap) occupies on the d-ring (Axiom 2). When two caps of half-angles $\alpha_1$ and $\alpha_2$ have their centers separated by $\theta$, the overlap region $A_{\text{overlap}}$ is determined by three auxiliary angles $\varphi_1, \varphi_2, \varphi_3$. Numerics: $f(\theta) = 1/2 - \varphi_1\cos\alpha_1/\pi - \varphi_2\cos\alpha_2/\pi - \varphi_3/(2\pi)$. Since this is a tool formula, error is not applicable (mathematically exact). Consistency: shared computational basis for H-98 (CAS cost cap), H-99 (lock-in ratio), and H-100 (Hopf projection). All three cards reference this formula. Physical correspondence: computation of the intersection region of two circles in spherical geometry. A standard formula used in astronomy (field-of-view overlap), crystallography (Brillouin zones), and so on. In existing mathematics this is an application of spherical trigonometry, but in Banya it is reinterpreted as a geometric expression of the CAS cost structure.
