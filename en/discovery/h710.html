Step 2. Norm Substitution

(a) The norm being substituted is the magneto-optic coupling norm, i.e. the Verdet constant $V$. (b) The lock-bit magnetic field gives the left- and right-circular polarizations different cost-propagation speeds (circular birefringence), and the phase difference of the two components accumulates along the path, so the rotation angle is substituted by a norm linear in the field $B$ and the path length $d$. Because the direction set by the field breaks time-reversal symmetry, the rotation is irreversible. Why this norm: the Faraday Angle itself originates from Lock-Bit-Magnetic-Field-Driven Polarization Rotation, so only a norm that carries that very cost structure maps onto it without slack. Why the substitution is valid: the standard physics form on the left and the Banya form on the right denote the same quantity, the latter merely rewriting it in CAS cost terms.

$\theta_F = V\,B\,d$
$V = \dfrac{\Delta(\text{lock-domain coupling})}{B}$
