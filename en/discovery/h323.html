H-323: Lorentz invariance = system time ≠ domain time mapping preservation

$$t_{\text{system}}\neq t_{\text{domain}};\;\text{Lorentz}=\text{mapping invariant}$$

Lorentz invariance is derived as preservation of the mapping between system time and domain time. System time (the δ utterance count) differs from domain time (the screen's time axis); the preservation of the mapping rule is Lorentz invariance.

Banya formula: $t_{\text{system}} \neq t_{\text{domain}}$. Lorentz transformation = when two observers map their respective domain time to system time, the mapping rule (transformation matrix) is preserved.

Axiomatic basis: Axiom 15 (δ = outside FSM, system time = δ utterance count) defines system time. Axiom 8 (screen) provides domain time (the rendered time axis). Axiom 1 (4 domain axes) prescribes the spacetime structure.

Structural consequence: system time is unobservable (δ is outside the FSM), so observers measure only domain time. Even if the two observers' domain times differ, the mapping rule to system time is identical. This is the structural meaning of Lorentz invariance.

Numerics: Lorentz factor $\gamma = \Delta t_{\text{system}}/\Delta t_{\text{domain}}$. $v/c = $ shift rate on the d-ring. $\gamma = 1/\sqrt{1-(v/c)^2}$ is the mapping ratio.

Physical correspondence: Lorentz invariance (the foundation of special relativity). The laws of physics take the same form in every inertial frame. Equivalent to the constancy of the speed of light.
