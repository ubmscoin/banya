Step 2. Norm Substitution

(a) The norm being substituted is the pipeline-pass norm that multiplies the per-step Compare passes. (b) It is chosen because ETL's extract-transform-load is isomorphic to the 5 steps of search-derivation-verification, and each step is judged independently by the Compare branch of Axiom 7. The total pass rate is therefore substituted by the product of the 5 per-step pass probabilities.

$P_\text{pass} = \prod_{i=1}^{5} p_i$
$P_\text{pass} = p^{5}$
