H-992. Koide Scale Deviation $Q(\mu) - 2/3 = \alpha(\mu)/(2\pi)$

Core Finding

H-992: the moment the Koide ratio leaves the pole-mass tier, it deviates by exactly one coupling spread over the reversible full circle $2\pi$

$$Q(\mu) - \tfrac{2}{3} = \frac{\alpha(\mu)}{2\pi}, \qquad Q(M_Z) - \tfrac{2}{3} = \frac{1}{2\pi \times 128} = \frac{1}{256\,\pi}$$

Hypothesis -- at $M_Z$: measured $+1.2580\times10^{-3}$ vs candidate $1.2440\times10^{-3}$ (1.1%). In the pure-QED window ($m_c$, $m_b$ scales): 0.2 to 0.8%

Paper IV (Koide), Section 5, predicted only the direction: mass is a domain-time quantity (grips recorded on DATA), so the Koide relation holds at pole masses and breaks in threshold-free schemes. This card supplies the size. With threshold-free (MS bar) masses, $Q$ deviates upward from $2/3$ by exactly the coupling divided by the reversible full circle $2\pi$ (norm reading of the Axiom 4 proposition; unit circle of the Axiom 1 proposition) -- the same form as the Schwinger term $a_e = \alpha/(2\pi)$, which the physics map registers as Compare cost over loop phase.

At $M_Z$ the nearest integer of $1/\alpha$ is $128 = 2^7$ (P-215), giving the framework-pure form $1/(256\pi)$. In the $137 = 128 + 9$ reading, raising the scale spends the residual cost 9 via Compare false, and one unit of coupling $1/128$ leaks onto the reversible amplitude circle.

Eight-scale falsification test. Using the Xing-Zhang-Zhou mass tables (arXiv 1112.3112), the required $1/\alpha$ obtained by inverting the formula falls monotonically 134.93 ($m_c$), 132.63 ($m_b$), 126.51 ($M_Z$), 125.38 ($m_t$), tracking the QED running (about 134.4, 132.3, 127.9). At the pole, $Q - 2/3 = -8\times10^{-6}$: the deviation vanishes. Exactly $2/3$ on the domain-time tier; one Schwinger quantum upon leaving it.

Re-substitution use: candidate for the open item (deviation size) of Paper IV Section 5. A scale-function layer distinct from D-14 (pole deviation, $\alpha^3$ order). Product of re-substituting P-215 and D-01.

Why it stops at hypothesis: 1.1% at $M_Z$ sits on the discovery boundary (1%). The comparison against actual $\alpha(\mu)$ is approximate (precise vacuum-polarization tables not yet collated), the regime above the electroweak scale (non-monotonic 1 TeV row) is unresolved, and 2-loop remains. The D-promotion path is closing these three.
