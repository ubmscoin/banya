(2) Banya starting point: Axiom 2 (CAS = Read, Compare, Swap). The atomicity of a CAS operation — only one succeeds at a time — is identical to the exclusivity of a fermion.

(3) Norm substitution: fermion = CAS(expected=0, new=1). Only the transition from empty slot (0) to occupied (1) is allowed. Boson = CAS(expected=N, new=N+1). Accumulation (N+1) on top of existing occupancy (N) is allowed.
