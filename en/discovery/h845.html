Core Finding

H-845: Integrated Circuit = ECS Parallel Array of Many CAS

$$N_{\text{transistors}} \sim 2^{(y-1971)/2} \;\;\text{(Moore's law = density increase of ECS-parallel CAS array)}$$

Grade: B

An integrated circuit (IC) is a structure in which many CAS (transistors) are arranged in parallel over a single ECS substrate. Each transistor = an independent CAS Compare unit. Wiring = cost-propagation path within the ECS. Moore's law = exponential growth of the CAS count per unit ECS area.

Axiom 2 (ECS substrate), Axiom 5 (CAS Compare parallelization), Axiom 7 (cost = power)

Axiom 2: the ECS lattice plays the role of substrate. A silicon wafer = a regular ECS. Axiom 5: parallel array of many CAS Compare units = logic gates, registers, ALU. Axiom 7: total cost = transistor count $\times$ unit switching cost. The power-density limit = the ECS heat-dissipation limit.

Logic gates: NAND, NOR = combinations of 2~4 CAS. Processor: array of $\sim 10^{10}$ CAS. Memory: regular CAS lattice. 3D stacking: multilayer ECS array. Power wall: increasing CAS density ==> heat-dissipation limit.

Modern IC: $\sim 10^{10}$~$10^{11}$ transistors. Process: $3$~$5\;\text{nm}$. Power: $\sim 100$~$300\;\text{W}$. Clock: $\sim 3$~$5\;\text{GHz}$.

Moore's law has held at $\sim 2\times$ every 2 years for 50 years. Recent slowdown.

Integrated circuit, VLSI, Moore's law, semiconductor process, CMOS technology.

Axiom 4 (cost) forces the total cost to be the product of the CAS count and the unit switching cost. The growth of CAS count per unit area is read in the time domain.

Step 2. Norm Substitution

The norm being substituted is the ECS parallel-array density norm. Integration density is the CAS count per unit area, and that count closes into the parallel CAS array on the ECS substrate of Axiom 2, so this norm is chosen. The transistor count is substituted by the product of the baseline-year array density and a doubling every two years. Why this norm: Moore's-Law Transistor Count itself originates from the ECS Parallel CAS Array, so only a norm that carries that very cost structure maps onto it without slack. Why the substitution is valid: the standard physics form on the left and the Banya form on the right denote the same quantity, the latter merely rewriting it in CAS cost terms.
