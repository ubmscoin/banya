In the muon absolute-lifetime formula the coefficient 192 emerges necessarily from the CAS structure. $192 = (2^3)^2 \times 3$, where $2^3 = 8$ is the number of d-ring bits (8-bit ring buffer) and 3 is the CAS stages (Read, Compare, Swap). $(2^3)^2 = 64$ is the square of the 8-bit ring buffer's state space — the number of cases formed by juim pairs on the d-ring. Multiplying this 64 by the 3 CAS stages yields $64 \times 3 = 192$, giving the coefficient of the Standard Model muon-lifetime formula. $G_F$ (Fermi constant) is the cost the CAS Swap S+1 generates at the weak-force vertex, and $G_F^2$ is the pairwise Swap. From the workbench viewpoint, the muon workbench's juida operation count is normalized to 192. Consistent with the lifetime ratio derived in D-50, this chains into D-52 (tau absolute lifetime).
