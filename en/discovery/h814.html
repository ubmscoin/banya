Core Finding

H-814: LSZ Reduction Formula = Free-Field Limit of CAS Input/Output States

$$\langle f|S|i\rangle \sim \prod_{\text{ext}} (p^2 - m^2) \cdot G_n \;\leftrightarrow\; \text{free-norm limit of FSM for CAS input/output}$$

Grade: C

The LSZ reduction formula is the procedure that extracts S-matrix elements from correlation functions by placing the external legs on the mass shell. In Banya, when the input/output states of CAS lie sufficiently far apart (free-field limit), the convergence of the FSM norm to the free norm is exactly the LSZ procedure.

Axiom 2 (CAS input/output), Axiom 6 (FSM free norm)

Axiom 2 (CAS = scattering operation), Axiom 6 (FSM norm = mass). When the input (initial state) and output (final state) of a CAS operation are sufficiently far from the interaction region, the FSM norm converges to its free (non-interacting) value. The $(p^2 - m^2)$ factor = projection onto the free FSM norm.

The LSZ formula requires that the free limit of CAS input/output be well-defined. This means CAS cost must have finite range (if cost spreads to infinity, the free limit is impossible). Under confinement, LSZ does not apply = CAS cost fails to reach the free limit.

QCD confinement scale: $\Lambda_{\text{QCD}} \approx 200\;\text{MeV}$.

Consistent with the scattering-experiment S-matrix.
