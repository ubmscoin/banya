5-Step Recursive Substitution: Deriving Quantum Entropy from d-ring State Mixedness

Step 1. Banya Formula

$$\delta^2 = (\text{time} + \text{space})^2 + (\text{observer} + \text{superposition})^2$$

Quantum entropy measures how mixed a d-ring state is. In Axiom 3 the d-ring is discrete and finite-dimensional, so entropy has a ceiling; in Axiom 2 each time a Compare fixes the superposition the mixedness drops and entropy decreases. A pure state (Compare done) is $S=0$; the undetermined maximally mixed state is the ceiling.

Step 2. Norm Substitution

(a) The norm being substituted is the von Neumann entropy norm of the d-ring density matrix — the Shannon measure of its eigenvalue distribution. (b) Why this norm: the information uncertainty of a CAS state is exactly the d-ring mixedness, and its maximum closes at $\ln d$ for the uniform distribution over dimension $d$ (H-595, max entropy $8\ln 2$). For an 8-bit d-ring, $d = 2^8$.

$S(\rho) = -\text{Tr}(\rho\ln\rho)$
$0 \;\leq\; S(\rho) \;\leq\; \ln d = 8\ln 2$
$S$: von Neumann entropy | $\rho$: d-ring density matrix | $0$: pure state (Compare done) | $\ln d$: maximally mixed ceiling | $d = 2^8$: 8-bit d-ring dimension
