H-443: Quantum Zeno effect = frequent Compare suppresses Swap

Compare false ==> no Swap (Axiom 7) ==> the Status transition "freezes." Observation does not cause collapse; frequent Compare deprives Swap of its opportunity.

5-Step Recursive Substitution: Deriving the Quantum Zeno Survival Probability from the δ Polling Period

Step 1. Banya Formula

$$\delta^2 = (\text{time} + \text{space})^2 + (\text{observer} + \text{superposition})^2$$

A Status transition advances only when δ polls every tick (Axiom 8) and Compare returns true so that a Swap occurs (Axiom 7). Reducing the polling interval to the extreme makes the Status change $\theta/n$ between Compares infinitesimal, and this is read as the polling-period cost of the time domain.

Step 2. Norm Substitution

The norm being substituted is the polling-period norm of the time domain. It is chosen because the Swap probability per Compare is $\sin^2(\theta/2n)$ and the survival probability is the $n$-fold product of $\cos^2(\theta/2n)$ (each Compare independent by CAS atomicity, Axiom 2). The single-transition probability of free evolution is therefore substituted by the product norm of $n$-split polling.

$P_{\text{survive}} = \cos^2\!\dfrac{\theta}{2}\quad(n=1,\ \text{free evolution})$
$P_{\text{survive}}(n) = \left(\cos^2\dfrac{\theta}{2n}\right)^{n}\ \xrightarrow[n\to\infty]{}\ 1$
$P_{\text{survive}}$: no-transition probability | $\theta$: total Rabi phase | $n$: number of polls (Axiom 8) | $\Delta t_{\text{poll}}=t/n$: polling period

Step 3. Constant Substitution

n = 256 Itano 1990 pulse count
$\theta$ = $\pi$ total transition phase
$P_{surv}$ = $\cos^{512}(\pi/512)$ n=256 accumulation
experiment = transition suppression $\sim$ 100% Itano 1990 (9-Be+)

Step 4. Domain Transformation

The transformation is from the free-evolution domain into the frequent-polling domain (time period split). What it reveals is how close the survival probability approaches 1 when the poll count $n$ is large. Substituting the Itano condition $n=256$ yields the value.

$P_{\text{survive}} = \cos^{512}\!\left(\dfrac{\pi}{512}\right) \approx 0.99$
