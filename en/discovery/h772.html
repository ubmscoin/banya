5-Step Recursive Substitution: Deriving the Equal Reality of DATA and OPERATOR from the Two Brackets of δ²

Step 1. Banya Formula

$$\delta^2 = (\text{time} + \text{space})^2 + (\text{observer} + \text{superposition})^2$$

The realism debate asks what is real. The Banya formula writes δ² as the sum of two brackets (Axiom 1). One bracket corresponds to the DATA substrate (Axiom 3: discrete entity), the other to the OPERATOR = CAS relations (Axiom 2). Since the two terms are added into δ², the realism debate is read not as an either/or but as the two terms of a single formula.

Step 2. Norm Substitution

The norm being substituted is the sum norm that adds the contributions of the two brackets of δ². Taking only one bracket as real (structural realism takes only DATA, relational quantum mechanics only CAS) forces discarding the contribution the other term still makes to δ², which is contradictory. The norm that adds the two brackets with equal weight is therefore chosen.

$\delta^2 = \underbrace{(\text{time}+\text{space})^2}_{\text{DATA: real substrate}} + \underbrace{(\text{observer}+\text{superposition})^2}_{\text{OPERATOR: relations}}$
$\text{real}(x) \iff [\,x\in\text{DATA}\,]\ \lor\ [\,x\in\text{OPERATOR}\,]$
DATA: discrete entity (Axiom 3) | OPERATOR: CAS relations (Axiom 2) | $\delta^2$: equal sum of two terms (Axiom 1) | real: reality predicate

Step 4. Domain Transformation

The transformation is into the observability domain. What it reveals is the reality status of objects that are not directly observed. The quark cannot be observed in a free state due to color confinement, but because it is recorded in DATA (Axiom 3) it is judged real in the Banya Framework. Observability is merely the access cost of the OPERATOR term and does not govern whether the DATA term is real.

$\text{real}(\text{quark}) = \text{true} \quad (\text{DATA record}),\qquad \text{observable}(\text{quark}) = \text{false}\ (\text{color confinement})$
