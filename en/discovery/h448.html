Core Finding

H-448: Entanglement = two entities as different projections of the same δ

$$|\Psi^-\rangle = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(|01\rangle - |10\rangle) \;\Longleftrightarrow\; \delta \xrightarrow{\text{proj}_A} |s_A\rangle,\; \delta \xrightarrow{\text{proj}_B} |s_B\rangle,\; s_A \oplus s_B = \text{const}$$

Grade: A

Two entities A and B being "entangled" means that they are both different projections (Axiom 11) of the same δ global flag (Axiom 15). Because δ exists outside the FSM, it is independent of spatial distance. When a Compare on A executes a Swap, the corresponding projection of δ is determined, and the projection of B is automatically fixed by the binding condition ($s_A \oplus s_B = \text{const}$). It is not signal transmission but reading different facets of the same flag.

Axiom 15 (δ = global flag outside FSM), Axiom 11 (multiple projection)

Axiom 15 (δ globality) ==> Axiom 11 (multiple projection, interaction strength) ==> Axiom 7 (Compare ==> Swap determines one side) ==> Axiom 10 (δ ==> observer loop)

Entanglement is not "spooky action at a distance" but two viewpoints on the single global variable δ. EPR paradox resolution: not a hidden variable, but a global flag outside the FSM. Faster-than-light communication impossible: Compare results (true/false) cannot be decoded without a classical channel (H-451).

Singlet Status: $P(+- \text{ or } -+) = 1$, $P(++ \text{ or } --) = 0$. Reproduces the statistics of every Bell experiment.
