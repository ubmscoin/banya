$$B(A,Z) = a_V A - a_S A^{2/3} - a_C Z^2 A^{-1/3} - a_A (A-2Z)^2/A + \delta \;\leftrightarrow\; \text{FSM cost reduction}$$

Each term of the Weizsäcker mass formula = cost contribution of FSM multi-binding. Volume term = cost reduction from FSM neighbor binding. Surface term = incomplete binding of boundary FSMs.
