Step 2. Norm Substitution

(a) The norm being substituted is the Holevo-information norm of CAS cost transfer — the von Neumann entropy of the output ensemble minus its conditional entropy. (b) Why this norm: the upper bound on classical information a quantum channel carries is exactly this quantity (the Holevo-Schumacher-Westmoreland theorem), and its maximum is the channel capacity. For a lossy bosonic channel, RLU attenuation fixes the mean photon number $\bar n$ and the capacity closes into the single function $g(\bar n)$.

$C_Q = \max_\rho \left[ S(\mathcal{E}(\rho)) - \sum_i p_i S(\mathcal{E}(\rho_i)) \right]$
$C_{\text{boson}} = g(\bar n) = (\bar n + 1)\log(\bar n + 1) - \bar n\log\bar n$
$C_Q$: quantum channel capacity | $S$: von Neumann entropy | $\mathcal{E}$: channel (CAS cost-transfer map) | $\rho$: input state | $\bar n$: mean photon number (set by RLU attenuation) | $g$: bosonic entropy function
