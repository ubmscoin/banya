Core Finding

H-708: Brillouin Scattering = CAS-Phonon Cost Exchange

$$\omega_s = \omega_i \pm \omega_{\text{phonon}} \;\leftrightarrow\; \text{CAS cost wave exchanging cost with acoustic phonons}$$

Grade: C

Brillouin scattering is the inelastic scattering in which a CAS cost wave exchanges cost with the acoustic phonons of a medium. Where Raman scattering involves optical phonons / molecular vibrations, Brillouin scattering is the exchange with acoustic phonons (long-wavelength lattice vibrations).

H-707 (Raman scattering), H-630 (phonon)

H-707 (inelastic cost exchange), H-630 (phonon = DATA lattice vibration), Axiom 4 (CAS-phonon cost coupling)

Brillouin shift $\sim \text{GHz}$ (small compared with Raman $\sim \text{THz}$). Sound-speed measurement: $v_s = \lambda\Delta\nu/(2n\sin(\theta/2))$. Determination of elastic constants. Stimulated Brillouin scattering (SBS).

Fused silica: Brillouin shift $\approx 34\;\text{GHz}$ ($\lambda = 532\;\text{nm}$).

Consistent with Brillouin spectroscopy experiments.

Brillouin scattering, acoustic-phonon spectroscopy, elastic-constant measurement, SBS.

Verified by Brillouin spectroscopy. SBS observation in optical fibers.

Where H-707 is exchange with optical phonons/molecular vibration, Brillouin is read as exchange with the acoustic phonons of H-630 (phonon = DATA lattice vibration) -- the long-wavelength lattice vibrations.

Step 2. Norm Substitution

(a) The norm being substituted is the frequency norm of the acoustic phonon. (b) The acoustic phonon is a low-frequency mode propagating through the lattice at the sound speed $v_s$, so the quantum the cost wave loses or gains is far smaller than the optical phonon of Raman (GHz vs THz), and the scattered frequency is substituted by a norm slightly offset from the incident line. The shift is fixed by the back-scattering geometry of the scattering angle $\theta$. Why this norm: the Brillouin Shift itself originates from CAS-Phonon Cost Exchange, so only a norm that carries that very cost structure maps onto it without slack. Why the substitution is valid: the standard physics form on the left and the Banya form on the right denote the same quantity, the latter merely rewriting it in CAS cost terms.

$\omega_s = \omega_i \pm \omega_{\text{phonon}}$
$\Delta\nu = \dfrac{2n v_s}{\lambda}\sin\!\dfrac{\theta}{2}$
$\omega_i$: incident cost wave | $\omega_s$: scattered cost wave | $\omega_{\text{phonon}}$: acoustic phonon (H-630) | $n$: refractive index | $v_s$: sound speed | $\theta$: scattering angle | $\lambda$: vacuum wavelength

(a) The transformation is into the Brillouin-spectroscopy domain (elastic-constant measurement).
