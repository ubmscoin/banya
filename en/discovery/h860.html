Step 2. Norm Substitution

(a) The norm being substituted is the screening-length norm defined by the ratio of thermal-motion energy to electrostatic energy. (b) This norm is chosen because the distance over which the liberated FSM population masks a charge perturbation is set solely by the balance of the two energies. The thermal energy $\varepsilon_0 k_B T_e$ is divided by the charge-density term $n_e e^2$, and the square root substitutes the length dimension. Why this norm: the Debye screening length itself originates from the FSM liberated state, so only a norm that carries that very cost structure maps onto it without slack. Why the substitution is valid: the standard physics form on the left and the Banya form on the right denote the same quantity, the latter merely rewriting it in CAS cost terms.

$\lambda_D = \sqrt{\dfrac{\varepsilon_0 k_B T_e}{n_e e^2}}$
$\omega_p = \sqrt{\dfrac{n_e e^2}{\varepsilon_0 m_e}}$
