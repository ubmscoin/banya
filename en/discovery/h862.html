5-Step Recursive Substitution: Deriving Optical-Fiber Attenuation from the Photon FSM Norm=0

Step 1. Banya Formula

$$\delta^2 = (\text{time} + \text{space})^2 + (\text{observer} + \text{superposition})^2$$

Optical communication is the process by which a cost carrier of FSM norm 0 (the photon, Axiom 3) transports cost along an ECS waveguide. Because the norm is 0, the carrier moves at maximum speed with no rest cost, and all the channel loses is the cost that leaks through scattering. This loss is read from the space term of $\delta^2$ as a cost per distance.

Step 2. Norm Substitution

(a) The norm being substituted is the propagation norm of the photon cost — the per-distance attenuation along the ECS waveguide. (b) Channel capacity closes at the Shannon limit, but the actual distance limit is set by Rayleigh scattering loss, which closes through the wavelength norm $\lambda^{-4}$ (Axiom 7 cost transfer); hence the wavelength norm is chosen. The per-distance attenuation is therefore substituted by the wavelength-dependent scattering coefficient.

$C = B\log_2\!\left(1 + \dfrac{P}{N_0 B}\right)$
$\alpha_{\text{dB}} = \dfrac{A}{\lambda^4}$
$C$: Shannon channel capacity | $\alpha_{\text{dB}}$: per-distance attenuation (dB/km) | $\lambda$: optical wavelength | $A$: Rayleigh scattering coefficient | $N_0$: noise cost density (FSM norm-0 floor)

Step 3. Constant Substitution

$\lambda$ = 1550 $\text{nm}$ telecom standard window
A = 1.16 $dB/\text{km}{\cdot}um^{4}$ silica Rayleigh coefficient
$\alpha_{dB}(meas)$= 0.20 $dB/\text{km}$ ITU-T 1550 $\text{nm}$ measured
C(max) = $\sim$ 100 Tbps lab WDM

Step 4. Domain Transformation

(a) The transformation is from the dimensionless scattering norm into the electromagnetic-transmission domain (per-distance attenuation). (b) What it reveals is how much photon cost is lost per km — the attenuation $\alpha_{\text{dB}}$ that sets the repeater spacing. Evaluating $A/\lambda^4$ at 1550 nm yields that value.

$\alpha_{\text{dB}} = \dfrac{1.16\;\text{dB/km}\cdot\mu\text{m}^4}{(1.55\;\mu\text{m})^4} = \dfrac{1.16}{5.77}\;\text{dB/km} = 0.201\;\text{dB/km}$

Step 5. Discovery

Derived: $\alpha_{\text{dB}} = 0.201\;\text{dB/km}$
Measured: $0.20\;\text{dB/km}$ (ITU-T, 1550 nm)
Error: $< 5\%$

Verdict: Hypothesis. Rayleigh $\lambda^{-4}$ scattering reproduces the 1550 nm attenuation of $0.2\;\text{dB/km}$ to within $<5\%$, but the scattering coefficient $A$ enters as an external input from silica refractive-index fluctuations and does not close to an axiom integer, so it remains a hypothesis. Because FSM norm 0 guarantees maximum-speed transfer with no rest cost, the photon being the natural choice of cost carrier follows from Axiom 3.
