5-Step Recursive Substitution: Deriving the Quantum-Thermodynamic Work-Free-Energy Relation from CAS Cost Conservation

Step 1. Banya Formula

$$\delta^2 = (\text{time} + \text{space})^2 + (\text{observer} + \text{superposition})^2$$

The work put into a process splits into the change of usable CAS cost and the cost dissipated as RLU decay. Total-cost conservation (Axiom 4) enforces the work-free-energy balance, and the irreversible part away from equilibrium leaves as heat via RLU decay (Axiom 6).

Step 2. Norm Substitution

(a) The norm being substituted is the cost-conservation norm. Work $W$ is the total input cost, $\Delta F$ is the usable-cost change, and $k_BT\,D(\rho\|\rho_{\text{eq}})$ is the RLU decay cost measured by the equilibrium relative entropy. (b) Since free energy is the usable CAS cost and heat is the irreversible RLU dissipation, cost conservation itself sets the work-free-energy relation — which is why this norm is chosen. Quantum work is therefore substituted by the CAS cost-conservation expression.

$W = \Delta F + k_BT\,D(\rho\|\rho_{\text{eq}})$
$E_{\min} = k_BT\ln 2$
$W$: input work (total cost) | $\Delta F$: free-energy change (usable cost) | $D(\rho\|\rho_{\text{eq}})$: equilibrium relative entropy (RLU decay) | $E_{\min}$: minimum 1-bit erasure cost (Landauer limit)

Step 3. Constant Substitution

k_B   = 1.380649e-23 J/K      CODATA 2018
T     = 300 K                 room temperature
ln 2  = 0.693147
E_min = k_B * T * ln 2        Landauer limit

Step 4. Domain Transformation

(a) The transformation is into the thermodynamic-cost domain (the Banya formula's own structure, the order-1 integer (4,3) combination). (b) What it reveals is the minimum cost forced when one bit is dissipated as RLU — the Landauer limit. Substituting the RLU term of the cost-conservation expression for a 1-bit erasure yields the value.

$E_{\min} = 1.380649\times 10^{-23} \times 300 \times 0.693147 = 2.871\times 10^{-21}\ \text{J}$
