(1) The Hard Problem of Consciousness (Chalmers) is the question "why is subjective experience accompanied by a physical process?" In the Banya Framework, this is a category error of trying to describe outside the FSM (δ) using the language inside the FSM.

(2) Banya formula: FSM language cannot describe δ ⇒ hard problem is category error. Physical processes are inside the FSM, but subjective experience is δ (outside the FSM).
