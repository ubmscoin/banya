Science Mining Method

Inventor: Han Hyukjin (bokkamsun@gmail.com)

Mining is a repeated procedure: start from the Banya equation and the axioms, derive physical constants and laws in the forward direction, judge them against measured values, and store the results in the library as inputs to the next derivation. The more results accumulate, the fewer unknowns remain.

1. Five-step recursive substitution

Every card is written in these five steps.

#StepWhat it does
1Banya equationStart from $\delta^2 = (\text{time} + \text{space})^2 + (\text{observer} + \text{superposition})^2$. The equation is never altered.
2Norm substitutionSubstitute the axes with variables of the target quantity and state why this norm.
3Constant substitutionInsert only measured constants and values from library cards (D, H, P). No hand-tuned free parameters.
4Domain conversionMove to the domain where the measurement is made and produce the number.
5FindingCompare with the measured value and give the verdict.
Axiom comparison rule: axioms are never summarized. Quote the supporting passage verbatim from the axiom text and check each clause as a constraint.

Card structure

One card consists of the sections below, in this order.

SectionWhat goes in it
CreatedThe date the card was first registered in the documents
Key findingOne-sentence conclusion, key formula, one line of precision, verbatim axiom passages used as grounds, re-substitution use (which cards it closes or corrects)

2. Verdicts and rejection rules

VerdictCondition
DiscoveryThe forward chain axiom ==> formula ==> number closes and matches the measured value within 1%. Graded S/A/B/C by necessity and precision.
HypothesisStructural correspondence confirmed; numerical derivation or formalization incomplete.
PredictionA value not yet measured, issued with falsification conditions.
RetiredFalsified, or internally inconsistent with other cards. The content is kept, with the reason for retirement attached.

Rejection rules:

  • No numerology: every number in a formula must come from the axiom structure. If "why this number" cannot be answered, the result is held back.
  • No circular reasoning: inserting a back-calculated measured value and deriving the same value is void.
  • Mark post-hoc fits: a reconstruction of an already known value is not called a prediction.
  • Keep byproducts: a structure visible despite large error stays on the card. It becomes the clue for the next round.

Numerology test example

The same formula can be rejected or accepted depending on its grounds. What is tested is the grounds, not the number. The Weinberg angle $\sin^2\theta_W = 7/(2+9\pi) = 0.23122$ is the example.

Initial verdict, rejected: the 9 in the denominator was claimed to be "the dimension of $SU(3)$", but $SU(3)$ is 8-dimensional. The grounds were wrong, so it was rejected.
Re-judged, accepted

All four numbers were re-obtained from the axiom structure. 7 = internal degrees of freedom (5 irreversible + 2 reversible), 2 = number of reversible axes (observer, superposition), 9 = residual cost (total cost 13 - ball value 4), $\pi$ = arc length of the irreversible hemisphere. Measured 0.23122 ± 0.00004 (PDG 2024, $\overline{\text{MS}}$, $M_Z$), relative deviation $4\times10^{-6}$.

3. Sample 1: the (5,2) signature of the fine-structure constant

$1/\alpha \approx 137$ is the constant Feynman called "a magic number that comes to us with no understanding by man." In 1969 Wyler reproduced it to several decimal places as a volume ratio of the symmetric space $SO_0(5,2)/[SO(5)\times SO(2)]$. But he could not answer the criticism "why $SO(5,2)$ and not another group" (Robertson, Gilmore), and for more than half a century the formula was treated as numerology. Mining forces this choice of group from the axioms.

Basis 1. Axiom text

Axiom 4 "Axes without order (observer, superposition) have no cost, no irreversibility, and no discrete steps."

Axiom 4 "Axes where CAS intervenes are irreversible (Axiom 2 Proposition: R==>C==>S reverse impossible, no refund), and axes where CAS does not intervene are non-irreversible. This binary verdict uniquely determines the cost accumulation structure of the CAS internal DOF 7 axes (Axiom 9)."

Basis 2. Partition

7 axes = 4 domain axes (time, space, observer, superposition) + 3 CAS stages (R, C, S). Assigning positive sign to the 5 irreversible axes (time, space, R, C, S) and negative sign to the 2 reversible axes (observer, superposition) gives the signature $(5,2)$. Of the 8 possible partitions of 7 axes, only $(5,2)$ and its isomorph $(2,5)$ are compatible with the axioms. $SO(7)$ and $SO(4,3)$, the other 21-dimensional groups, are excluded.

Basis 3. Number
$$\frac{1}{\alpha} = \frac{8\pi^4}{9}\left(\frac{2^4 \cdot 5!}{\pi^5}\right)^{1/4} = 137.036\,082$$

Measured 137.035 999 177 (CODATA 2022), relative error $6\times10^{-7}$. Zero free parameters.

Re-substitution chain

A closed result becomes the input to the next derivation. In the same paper, $\alpha$ and $\sin^2\theta_W$ are inserted to produce the baryon-to-photon ratio $\eta_B$ of the universe ("why one baryon survived per roughly $10^9$ photons").

$\eta_B$ is a quantity for which the Standard Model has no settled mechanism producing its number. As measurement precision improves, this value will be judged right or wrong.

4. Sample 2: retirement and prediction

Mining discards its own wrong cards. Inside the library, P-167 (the neutrino is a Dirac particle) contradicted H-550, H-797, P-357 (Majorana premise). The axioms decided.

Path 1. Axioms

The neutrino is the particle that skips Compare (H-05 "Compare skip = no lock acquired"). Axiom 5 "when the lock enforces order, simultaneity breaks and sequential access results." Axiom 4 "Axes without order (observer, superposition) have no cost, no irreversibility, and no discrete steps." No lock means no order, no order means reversible, and by the level-spacing class proposition of Axiom 4 a reversible operation is of real class. The real class returns to itself under conjugation, so particle = antiparticle: Majorana.

Path 2. Library

Neutrinos are left-handed only (H-31) and have no light right-handed partner (P-28, P-165). A Dirac mass needs a left-handed and a right-handed component paired, so it cannot form.

RetiredP-167 (Dirac), P-47 ($m_{ee}\sim0.1$ meV) Retired Content kept, with the reason and date of retirement attached.
ReplacementP-641 the neutrino is Majorana, $m_{\beta\beta} \in [1.47,\ 3.67]$ meV (normal ordering, $m_1 = 0$: P-185) Prediction
Falsification(1) If a next-generation ton-scale experiment detects $m_{\beta\beta} \gt 5$ meV, the range is discarded. (2) If an experiment with sensitivity below 1 meV sees no signal, the Majorana prediction is discarded. (3) If a light right-handed neutrino is confirmed, path 2 fails.

5. Library accumulation

A judged result is written as one card and registered as one row in the hypothesis library (discoveries, hypotheses) or in predictions (predictions). The next mining run cites that card number in step 3 (constant substitution) to insert its value. One discovery is the input to the next.
